On location

Basil Lane, the gazebo

Founded in 2019, Brandhouse is a one-director video studio run by Tyler Boyle, making brand films, commercials, wedding films and documentary work across Louisiana and the Gulf Coast.

Mission

Brandhouse exists to make video that earns attention instead of buying it. Tyler directs, shoots and edits most projects himself, with a small bench of trusted crew for bigger days. That keeps the voice consistent from first call to final export.

We work with businesses who have a real story and couples who want a film they will still watch in twenty years. If a project needs a drone, a crane, or a second unit, we bring it. If it needs one camera and good light, we bring that instead.

Services

Brand films and commercials. Concept, script, production and post. Thirty-second spots up to three-minute films, delivered in every aspect ratio you need.

Wedding films. Highlight films from four to eight minutes, plus full ceremony and speeches. Two shooters on request.

Documentary and interview. Founder stories, nonprofit campaigns, long-form interviews lit and recorded properly.

Real estate and architecture. Twilight walkthroughs, aerials and agent intros. Fast turnaround for listings.

Studio hire. The stage, lighting package and colour suite are available by the half day.
