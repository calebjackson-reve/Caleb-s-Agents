Under the Oak

Directed byTyler Boyle
LocationSt. Francisville, Louisiana
Year2026
Format2 clips, 16:9 and 9:16, AI-assisted
▶

A walled courtyard with one enormous live oak at its centre. The venue needed to sell the night before it had hosted one, so we started from a daylight frame of the real courtyard and built a single continuous move that carries it from afternoon into string lights and lanterns.

Alongside it, a vertical dolly toward the trunk cut for Reels, where the string lights come up as the camera settles. Both clips loop cleanly and run without sound on the venue's site and socials.

The day-to-night move was generated with AI from a single real frame of the courtyard, then graded in Resolve. Say so on every post. Tap any clip to play.
