Basil Lane

Directed byTyler Boyle
LocationSt. Francisville, Louisiana
Year2026
Format4 clips, 16:9, AI-assisted motion
▶

A white farmhouse with a deep timber porch on Basil Lane in St. Francisville. The listing photography was strong, so instead of reshooting we gave each still a single slow move: a push-in on the house at blue hour, a glide along the pool deck at dusk, a drift toward the gazebo on the pond, a locked-off frame on the primary bath with the fireplace lit.

Every move was written to protect the real property. No new objects, no people, no changed materials. Water, flames, leaves and window light are the only things that move. The clips run four to eight seconds each and cut together into a thirty-second listing film or run on their own as social posts.

Motion was generated with AI from the real listing stills, then graded and conformed in Resolve. Say so on every post. Tap any clip to play.
