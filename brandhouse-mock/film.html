Long Road Home

Directed byTyler Boyle
ClientBayou Trucks (sample)
Year2026 (Louisiana)
Runtime2 min 10, and a 60 sec cut
▶

A sixty-second spot and a two-minute cut for a regional truck dealer, shot over one evening on Highway 1. Practical lighting, one picture car and a chase vehicle. The brief was simple: make the drive home feel like the best part of the day.

We scouted three stretches of road and picked the one with the longest clean horizon. Sunset gave us twenty-two usable minutes, so every setup was rehearsed in daylight first. Sound was recorded in the cab with two lavs and a 416 on the dash.

Delivered in 16:9, 9:16 and 1:1 with a broadcast-safe mix and a social mix. The 60 second cut ran on local TV for six weeks. Sample copy for this mock.
