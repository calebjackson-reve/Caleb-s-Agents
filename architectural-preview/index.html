Living · golf and water

Live near where
you play.

Six courses within an easy drive of Zachary, and what the neighborhoods around them actually trade like. Ask me what it costs to live on any of them.

[01]Copper Mill Golf ClubZachary · Public

The neighborhood I have closed in most often. Golf-cart streets, newer construction, HOA.

[02]Beaver Creek Golf ClubZachary · Public

Parish course on the north side. Acreage and older homes nearby, no HOA in most of it.

[03]The Bluffs on Thompson CreekSt. Francisville · Private

Arnold Palmer design. Lake lots and the Feliciana hills, thirty minutes from Zachary on Highway 61.

[04]Santa Maria Golf CourseBaton Rouge · Public

Robert Trent Jones Jr. design in southeast Baton Rouge. Established family neighborhood around it.

[05]University ClubBaton Rouge · Private

Gated, newer luxury construction, and the long commute north if you work in Zachary.

[06]Carter PlantationSpringfield · Public

David Toms design across the Amite in Livingston Parish. A different parish for taxes and schools.

Membership, hours and HOA rules change. Confirm before you drive. Price ranges come from the MLS when you ask. Nothing here is invented.

False River, Thompson Creek,
the Comite.

False River in New Roads is a ten-mile oxbow lake with camps and full-time homes on both sides, forty minutes from Zachary across the Audubon Bridge. Thompson Creek runs the Feliciana line below the Bluffs.

The Comite is the river Zachary drains to, and the diversion canal finishing in 2028 is the biggest change to north Baton Rouge flooding in a generation.
