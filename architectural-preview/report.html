    This is screening, not a flood determination. FEMA's maps are the source and they are redrawn; a surveyor's elevation certificate is what a lender and an insurer actually price from. Zone at a point can differ from the zone across a parcel.

    Why this is the first question.

    In 2016, about half the East Baton Rouge homes that flooded sat outside the high-risk zone. The maps were not wrong so much as old, and the water did not read them. That is why a zone X result here still ends with "get the quote" instead of "you are fine."

    The Comite diversion canal, finishing in 2028, is the biggest change to north Baton Rouge flooding in a generation. If you are buying anywhere it drains, that is worth a conversation before you write an offer.
