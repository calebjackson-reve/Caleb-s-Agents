## Guardrails

1. **RESPA comes first for title companies.** Federal RESPA Section 8 limits things of value passing between settlement service providers and agents tied to referrals. Being paid by a title company you also send business to could be a problem. Get a real estate attorney's written opinion before any paid work with a title or escrow company. Keep it separate from referral relationships.
2. **Brokerage policy.** Confirm KW First Choice rules on outside businesses, using the brokerage name, and recruiting before teaching other agents.
3. **Client data.** Teach that nonpublic personal and financial information stays out of AI tools unless the company's own approved setup allows it. Title companies handle data protected by federal privacy law. Wire instructions never go through AI.
4. **No overpromising.** No guaranteed results. Time-saved claims come from pilot measurements.
5. **Approval gate.** AI drafts, a person approves anything sent to a client.
6. **Branding.** Keep AIRE separate from KW First Choice branding unless the brokerage approves. No Rêve materials.
