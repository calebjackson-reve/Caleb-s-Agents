## Ethics check on every lesson (confirmed rule, Caleb, 2026-10-14)

Caleb asked that nothing taught here creates an ethics violation. Every script gets this check before filming. Code article numbers are from Claude's knowledge of the NAR Code of Ethics (historical, recheck against the current year's Code). This is a plain-language read, not legal advice. KW First Choice compliance and the Louisiana Real Estate Commission rules have the final word.

| Risk | Where it shows up | Rule we teach |
|---|---|---|
| Client confidentiality (NAR Article 1) | Lead hunt, CRM | Never paste a client's private details into AI. Black out first. |
| Truth in advertising (Article 2 and 12) | Bio, video, website, ads | AI copy gets fact checked by the agent. No made up stats, awards or reviews. Brokerage name shown where required (Article 12 standard on firm name). |
| Equal service and Fair Housing (Article 10, federal and state Fair Housing law) | Every prompt, contact sorting, ads | Sort by relationship only. No protected traits, no "good schools" or neighborhood demographics talk, Meta housing ad category. |
| Clients of other agents (Article 16) | Lead hunt, follow-ups | If the scan finds someone already working with another agent, the lesson says leave them alone. |
| Talking about other agents (Article 15) | Content, posts | No false or misleading claims about competitors. |
| Fake or deepfake content | AI video, avatars | Disclose AI voice or avatar. Never fake a testimonial or a client. |
| Texting and email consent (TCPA, Do Not Call, CAN-SPAM) | Automations | No automated texts or calls without written consent. Every email has an opt out. |
| Brokerage policy | Everything | Teach "check your broker's AI policy" in Setup. KW First Choice answer still open. |

## FREE: 7 Days to Find the Leads in Your Phone (draft, phone first, ChatGPT)

| Day | Title (working) | They do | Psychology idea (inference, Claude's pick) | They post in the feed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome | Watch 90 seconds, say hi | Saying it out loud makes you follow through. A public "Day 0" is a small commitment. | "Day 0, here's my market" |
| 1 | Set Up Your Phone | ChatGPT and Claude apps, accounts, pay for one (about $20, historical), two-factor, training off | Friction decides more than motivation. Make starting take 10 minutes. | A screenshot of the apps on their home screen |
| 2 | What Never Goes In | Your AI rules card, ethics and Fair Housing basics, "ask your broker" step. Saved into ChatGPT's custom instructions so every chat follows it | People decide to trust you by what you protect, not what you say. | Their rules card |
| 3 | Practice on Fake Texts | Paste a sample thread Caleb provides (made up people) and have ChatGPT sort it. Safe practice before real data | Fear of a mistake stops people more than lack of skill. Fake data makes the mistake free. | What surprised them |
| 4 | The Scavenger Hunt | Scroll the last 90 days of texts, pick the work threads, black out money and private details, paste them in | Your next deal is usually someone who already knows you. Familiar beats cold. | How many work threads they found (a number, no names) |
| 5 | Who's Who | Sort every person: friend, family, agent, client, vendor. Relationship only, never protected traits. Flag anyone already working with another agent and leave them alone | The same words land differently from a friend, a vendor or an agent. Context changes the decision. | Their counts by group |
| 6 | The Follow-Up They Answer | Pick 3 people who need a follow-up. ChatGPT drafts a text in your voice. You edit and send it yourself | People answer messages about them, not about you. Specific beats "just checking in." | One reply they got (with permission, no names) |
| 7 | Your Saved Lead Assistant | A saved ChatGPT project with the sorting prompt and rules, so the hunt runs again every week in 10 minutes. Teaser for the paid CRM build | Systems beat willpower. A weekly 10-minute habit outlasts a big push. | "Day 7 done" |

### Lead hunt details and flags

- Phone first method (inference, needs a live test): an iPhone app can't read your whole iMessage history, so the member picks and pastes threads.
- Full scan method, Mac bonus: OpenAI launched an Apple Messages plugin for ChatGPT around 2026-08-20. It runs locally, needs Full Disk Access, can search, draft and send texts (historical, TechCrunch 2026-08-20, not tested). Reports say Mac (inference, iPhone support not mentioned). Claude also lists a "Read and Send iMessages" connector (historical, claude.com connectors page, not tested). Taught as a bonus with the same black out and consent rules, and with sending approval left on so nothing goes out without the agent reading it.
- The people in those texts never agreed to have an AI read them. Scan the narrowest set: last 90 days, work threads only.
- No loan amounts, account numbers, addresses tied to money, or health details go in. Project rule: no client financial data in AI.
- Sort by relationship only. Never by race, religion, family status, disability, national origin, sex or any protected trait.
- Feed posts share numbers and wins, never names or screenshots of real texts.

## PAID LATER: the build path (Claude)

### Build 1. Your own CRM (Google Sheets)
Turn the lead hunt list into a Google Sheet CRM. Follow-up dates, stage, source. Template download.

### Build 2. Videos with AI
Scripts with AI, filming on your phone, AI editing. AI video and avatars with a disclosure rule. Fair Housing check on every video and caption.

### Build 3. Your own website
A free site Claude builds and hosts, your own domain, contact form that drops leads into the CRM. Brokerage name and license info where required (inference, check Louisiana rules and KW policy).

### Build 4. Lead tools and automations
Home value request, seller net sheet, buyer checklist. New lead lands in the CRM, gets a reply, gets a reminder. No automated texts without written consent (TCPA, inference, not legal advice).

### Build 5. Analytics and ads
Read your numbers on the site and on social. Meta requires the housing Special Ad Category, which limits age, gender and zip targeting (historical, recheck). One small test ad, budget set by the agent.
