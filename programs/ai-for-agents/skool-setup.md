## Group description

> Learn AI from zero, built for real estate agents. Short lessons, real examples, and the psychology of why clients decide what they decide. No tech background needed. Hosted by Caleb Jackson, a REALTOR in Louisiana.

## Join questions (Skool asks these when people request to join)

1. Are you a licensed real estate agent? Where?
2. What's the one task that eats most of your week?
3. Have you used AI before? (Never, a little, weekly)

These answers tell you what to teach next.

## Sections

**Community categories**
- Start Here (pinned welcome post)
- Wins (share what AI saved you this week)
- Questions
- Prompts that worked

**Classroom**
- Module 1: The Basics (Lessons 1 to 3)
- Module 2: Talking to Clients (Lessons 4 and 5)
- Module 3: Content and Video (Lessons 6 and 8)
- Module 4: Mindset and Safety (Lessons 7 and 9)
- Module 5: Your First Assistant (Lesson 10)

**Calendar**
- Fridays: live Q&A, 30 to 45 minutes. Pick the time you can keep every week.

## Rules

1. Be kind. Everyone starts at zero.
2. No selling or recruiting in posts.
3. Never post client personal or financial information.
4. Share what works. Ask anything.
