## Rules every message follows
Your state's advertising rules go here. In Louisiana: name and brokerage on every piece, real sender, opt-out on every email, no cold texts, no price or timing guarantees, fair housing language only. Ask your broker for yours and write it in. If you don't know a rule, write "ask broker" so nothing goes out on a guess.
