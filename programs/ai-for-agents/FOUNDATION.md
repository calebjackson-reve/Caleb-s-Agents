# AI for Agents: Foundation

**Status: draft for Caleb.** These are my best read of what you've told me and how you work. Change any word that doesn't sound like you. Once you approve, every script, post and lesson gets checked against this page.

## The goal

**100 free Skool members by day 90. Every one of them a real agent who used AI for something real.**

After that, in order:
1. One-on-one sessions for members who want more help (paid, starting in week 5).
2. A paid tier after 100 members, at $27 to $49 a month (see `skool-research.md`).
3. AIRE grows out of what members actually use.

Why 100 and not 10,000: AI Automation Society has 470,000 members and 89% never post (see `skool-teardown-ais-chase.md`). We'd rather have 100 agents who show up than 1,000 who lurk.

## The mission in one line

**Help agents use AI so they think better, not just faster.**

## Who it's for

- Licensed agents, starting in Louisiana, who feel behind on AI or have tried it and gave up.
- Agents who are good with people and don't want to become "tech people."
- Not for: people looking to start an AI agency, lead-buying schemes, or anyone who wants AI to do the job for them.

## The promise

In 7 days, starting from zero, you'll have a working AI assistant that writes like you, plus 6 things it already did for your business.

## Core values

1. **Step 1 first.** I once lost a room of agents in five minutes because I started at step 50. Every lesson starts where the newest agent is.
2. **Think better, not just faster.** AI is a thinking partner. We teach the psychology of why clients decide, and AI helps us apply it. Speed is the side effect.
3. **You're the agent. It's the assistant.** AI drafts. You check every fact, edit and send. No autopilot with clients.
4. **Client trust comes first.** No client personal or financial info in AI. Fair Housing on every motivation question. No spam.
5. **Show the work.** Real listings, real prompts, real mistakes on screen. No polished fake results, no income flexing.
6. **Do one thing today.** Every video ends with one action you can do in 10 minutes. Watching isn't learning.
7. **Give first.** The lessons, templates and challenge are free. People pay for my time, not for access to basics.

## How we get there (the flywheel)

1. **Film** one long lesson a week for YouTube, plus the 7-day challenge once.
2. **Cut** 3 to 5 short clips from each lesson for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
3. **Every video points to one place:** "The free template is in my Skool group."
4. **Personal Facebook invites** to 10 to 15 agents a week, by hand.
5. **New members do the 7-day challenge** and post their builds in the feed.
6. **Their wins become proof posts** (with permission), which bring in the next agents.
7. **Friday live Q&A** answers what they're stuck on, and those questions become next week's lessons.

## What we never do

- Post, send or pay for anything without Caleb's approval.
- Use Rêve branding. Use the Keller Williams First Choice name only as brokerage policy allows.
- Fake urgency, flash sales or "price going up" countdowns.
- Mass messaging tools on Facebook.
- Mental health advice. Mindset content is perspective. Point to professional help and 988 when depression comes up.

## How to check any piece of content

Before it goes out, it should pass all five:
1. Could a brand new agent follow it?
2. Does it have one clear action at the end?
3. Is there a psychology idea, not just a tool trick?
4. Does it point to Skool?
5. Does it sound like Caleb talking, not like AI?
