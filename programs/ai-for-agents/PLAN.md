Caleb Jackson teaches real estate agents how to use AI from zero, mixed with the psychology of why people decide what they decide.

## How the pieces connect

1. **YouTube:** one long lesson a week, talking to camera plus screen share.
2. **Short clips:** 3 to 5 cut from each lesson for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok.
3. **Personal Facebook:** where agents see you. Posts plus personal invites.
4. **Free Skool group:** the beginner path in order, a weekly live Q&A, and a place to ask questions.
5. **Later, paid:** one-on-one sessions now, a paid tier after 100 members.

Every video ends with the same ask: *"The full beginner path is free in my Skool group. Link's in the description."*

## The 90 days

### Weeks 1 and 2: Get ready (no launch yet)
- Ask KW First Choice about outside business rules and using the brokerage name in teaching.
- Pick the Skool group name and set it up from `skool-setup.md`. Skool's Hobby plan is $9/month with a 10% fee on paid memberships (checked 2026-10-12, see `skool-research.md`). Approve before signing up.
- Set up the YouTube channel: name, photo, banner, one-line description.
- Film Lessons 1, 2 and 3 so you launch with a library.
- Draft your one-on-one offer: what it covers and your price.

### Weeks 3 and 4: Soft launch (target: 25 members)
- Publish Lessons 1 to 3 on YouTube and Skool.
- Post the launch announcement on personal Facebook (draft in `invites.md`).
- Send personal invites to your 50 closest agent friends.
- First Friday live Q&A.

### Weeks 5 to 8: Build the habit (target: 60 members)
- One lesson a week (Lessons 4 to 7).
- 10 to 15 personal invites a week.
- Ask your first members what they are stuck on. Turn answers into short videos.
- Offer one-on-one sessions to members who ask for more help.
- Pitch one live talk: your KW office meeting, or a lender or title company agent event (check RESPA first if money changes hands).

### Weeks 9 to 12: Push to 100 (target: 100 members)
- Lessons 8 to 10.
- Member wins post: share 2 or 3 member results, with permission.
- Second personal invite round to agents who engaged but didn't join.
- Week 12: review numbers, decide on the paid tier.

## Guardrails

1. **Approval gate.** Claude drafts. Caleb posts, sends and pays.
2. **Fair Housing.** Motivation questions stay on timeline, money and goals. Never steer on protected traits.
3. **Client data.** Never paste client financial info, Social Security numbers or wire details into AI. This is taught in Lesson 9.
4. **Mental health.** Mindset content is perspective, not treatment. When depression comes up, point people to professional help, including the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US).
5. **RESPA.** Get a real estate attorney's opinion before any paid deal with a title company or lender.
6. **Facebook limits.** Send invites by hand, a few at a time. No mass messaging tools.
7. **Branding.** Caleb Jackson personal brand. Keller Williams First Choice only as brokerage policy allows. No Rêve.
