# Day 0: Welcome

**7 Days to Find the Leads in Your Phone**

About 90 seconds · Face first, phone in hand · Also the Skool about video and a Facebook and Instagram clip

Status: draft for Caleb, 2026-10-14. Not approved. Nothing posted.

Psychology idea for today: saying it out loud makes you follow through. A public "Day 0" post is a small commitment, and small commitments turn into finished challenges (inference, Claude's framing of the commitment and consistency effect).

## The first 3 seconds (pick one)

| | A (recommended) | B | C |
|---|---|---|---|
| First words | "There's probably a deal sitting in your texts right now. You just haven't seen it." | "You read this text back in March. And you never answered it." | "You don't need more leads. You need the ones already in your phone." |
| Shot | Tight on Caleb's face, he lifts his phone into frame, screen toward lens | Full screen phone recording of a made-up text thread, one line lights up | Medium shot, Caleb scrolling his phone, then looks up into the lens on "need" |
| Angle | Curiosity. Every agent has texts they forgot. | Guilt they recognize in one second | Contrarian, flips the "more leads" pitch every coach sells |
| On-screen text | YOUR NEXT DEAL IS ALREADY IN YOUR PHONE | "we might sell in the spring 🤔" (highlighted) | STOP BUYING LEADS. FIND THEM. |
| "Me too" line | "That friend who said they might move. The past client who asked one question. Buried under four thousand other texts." | Same line as A | Same line as A |

Why A: it promises something the viewer already owns, so it costs them nothing to keep watching. B is the strongest visual but opens on screen, and the film kit rule is face first. C is punchy but reads like an ad for a lead company.

"Probably" stays in the hook on purpose. A flat promise of a deal would be an overclaim (truth in advertising, see ethics check).

## Script (hook A)

**[0:00 Tight, phone into frame]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: YOUR NEXT DEAL IS ALREADY IN YOUR PHONE

There's probably a deal sitting in your texts right now. You just haven't seen it.

**[0:04 B-roll: made-up text bubbles scrolling fast]**
That friend who said they might move. The past client who asked one quick question. The lender who sent you a name. All buried under four thousand other texts.

**[0:12 Back to face, medium]**
For the next 7 days, we're going to dig them out. With AI. On your phone.

**[0:16 B-roll: 7-day strip, each day lights up as he says it]**
Day 1, we set up your phone. Day 2, your rules for what never goes into AI. Day 3, you practice on fake texts I give you, so your first try isn't on a real client. Day 4, the scavenger hunt. Day 5, you sort who's who. Day 6, you send three follow-ups. Day 7, you save it so it runs every week.

**[0:38 Punch-in 115%]**
Ten to twenty minutes a day. If you can text, you can do this.

You'll want one AI app on the paid plan. About twenty bucks a month. I'll show you which one on Day 1.

**[0:48 Back to medium]**
And we do this the right way. No client money details go in. Nobody gets sorted by anything but how you know them. Your clients' trust is the whole business.

**[0:56 Punch-in]**
Post in the group right now. Write "Day 0," where you sell, and how many unread texts you have. Number only, no names.

People who post Day 0 finish. Say it out loud.

**[1:06 Back to wide]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 1 is waiting in the Classroom
Day 1 is ten minutes. I'll see you there.

About 230 spoken words. Lands near 1:30 at a normal pace (inference, not timed). If it runs long, cut the price line first.

## Ethics check

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Truth in advertising (NAR Article 12) | Hook says "probably," not a promise. No income or deal claims. |
| Client confidentiality (Article 1) | No real texts on screen. The feed post asks for a number, never names. |
| Fair Housing | No talk about who should live where. "Might move" and "looking in Lafayette" describe a person's own plan, not a group. |
| Price claim | "About twenty bucks" is historical, recheck the ChatGPT and Claude plan prices on filming week. |
| Brokerage name | Not used. Waiting on KW First Choice. |
| AI disclosure | Nothing AI generated stands in for Caleb or a client. Animations are graphics. |
