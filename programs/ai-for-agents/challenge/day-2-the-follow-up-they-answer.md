# Day 2: The Follow-Up They Answer

About 6 minutes · Build: 3 follow-up texts · Post as `Day 2: follow-ups`

## Shot list
- On camera: open, psychology idea, close.
- Screen: same chat as Day 1, or a new one.
- B-ROLL: your phone with a text thread (fake names only), the "read" receipt.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 2

✂ CLIP START
Most follow-up texts don't get answered for one reason. They're about us.

"Just checking in!" "Let me know if you have any questions!"

People reply when two things are true. You gave them something first. And replying takes two seconds.

That's reciprocity. Give before you ask. Then make the answer one word.
✂ CLIP END

Today AI helps you write three of those.

**[Screen]**
Same six pieces as yesterday. Who you are. What you need. Who it's for. The details. How it should sound. How long.

Type this:

> I'm a real estate agent in [your area]. Write 3 follow-up texts. One for someone who came to my open house on [street name, no address number] last weekend. One for an online lead who asked about a home 3 weeks ago and went quiet. One for a past client I helped buy 2 years ago. Each text should give them something useful first, like a recent sale nearby or a quick market note, and end with a question they can answer in one word. Sound like a friendly text, not an email. Under 40 words each. No exclamation points.

Enter.

**[Screen, reading]**
Read the open house one. It gives them something, then asks an easy question.

Now make it yours. Type: "Make the second one more casual. I'd never say 'reach out.'"

B-ROLL: you texting on your phone.

✂ CLIP START
**[On camera]**
Here's the rule. AI drafts. You read it, fix anything that's not true, and send it yourself. And never paste a client's phone number, email or anything personal into AI. First names are as far as it goes. We'll go deeper on that on Day 6.
✂ CLIP END

Your build for today: three follow-up texts, edited in your voice. Post them in the group as Day 2. Bonus points if you send one today and tell us if they replied.
