# Day 7: Your Saved Assistant

About 8 minutes · Build: a saved Claude Project or ChatGPT setup · Post as `Day 7: done 🎓`

## Shot list
- On camera: open, the "why" and the graduation close.
- Screen: creating a Claude Project, with the ChatGPT version shown briefly.
- B-ROLL: you at your desk, phone in hand, a closing table.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 7

All week you've told AI who you are, over and over. Today you do it once. And it remembers.

✂ CLIP START
Here's why this matters. Good habits stick when they're easy. If you have to explain yourself every time, you'll stop using it. If it already knows you, you'll use it every day without thinking.
✂ CLIP END

**[Screen]**
I'll show you Claude, then the same thing in ChatGPT.

In Claude, click Projects, then New project. Name it "My Real Estate Assistant."

Click to add instructions. This is where everything from this week goes. Paste this and fill it in:

> About me: I'm [name], a real estate agent in [area] with [brokerage, as your policy allows]. I mostly help [who].
> How I sound: [paste your Day 1 bio]. Warm, plain, short sentences. Never use words like stunning, nestled or dream home.
> What I use you for: follow-up texts, consult questions, listing video scripts, social posts.
> My rules: [paste your 6 rules from Day 6].
> Always: ask me for details before writing something personal. Tell me when you're not sure about a fact.

Save.

Now test it. In a new chat inside the project, type just: "Follow-up text for an open house visitor from Saturday."

**[Screen, reading]**
Look at that. I didn't tell it who I am, where I sell or how I sound. It already knew.

In ChatGPT, it's Settings, then Personalization, then Custom instructions. Same text. Or make a project there too.

**[On camera]**
✂ CLIP START
A week ago, you were starting at step 1. Today you have an assistant that knows your business and writes like you. That's not behind. That's ahead of most agents.
✂ CLIP END

Post your test as Day 7, with "done." I'll add you to the Graduates list, and I'll shout you out on Friday's live.

From here, there's a new lesson every week, and I'm live every Friday to answer questions. If you want help building out more of your system, ask me about a one-on-one.

You did it. I'm proud of you.
