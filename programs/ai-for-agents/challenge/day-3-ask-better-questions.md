# Day 3: Ask Better Questions

About 7 minutes · Build: your 5 consult questions · Post as `Day 3: my questions`

## Shot list
- On camera: most of this one. The psychology is the lesson.
- Screen: the prompt and result.
- B-ROLL: a kitchen table, a notepad, a front door.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 3

✂ CLIP START
When someone tells you why they're moving, that's almost never the real reason.

"We need more space." Okay. But why now? What happened?

Maybe a baby's on the way. Maybe a job. Maybe they just can't stand the commute one more year. The first answer is the polite one. The real one is two questions deeper.

And if you only hear the first answer, you'll show them the wrong houses.
✂ CLIP END

Today AI helps you write the questions that get to the real reason.

One line before we start, because it matters. We ask about timeline, money and goals. Never about who someone is. No questions about family status, religion, race, national origin, disability or anything else Fair Housing protects. And you never steer anyone toward or away from a neighborhood because of who they are.

TEXT ON SCREEN: Timeline · Money · Goals. Never who they are.

**[Screen]**
Type this:

> I'm a real estate agent in [your area]. Help me write 5 open-ended questions for a first meeting with buyers. The goal is to understand their real motivation and timeline, not just what they say first. Each question should invite a story, not a yes or no. Include one follow-up question for each, for when their answer is vague. Stay Fair Housing compliant: only ask about timeline, budget, goals and the home itself, never about protected traits. Keep it conversational.

Enter.

**[Screen, reading]**
Look at this one. "What's changed in the last few months that made now the time?" That's a story question. They can't answer it with one word.

Now ask it to check itself. Type: "Double-check these for anything that could be a Fair Housing problem."

B-ROLL: kitchen table, notepad.

✂ CLIP START
**[On camera]**
Here's what's really happening. You're not using AI to talk to the client. You're using it to think before you walk in the door. That's what I mean by think better, not just faster.
✂ CLIP END

Your build for today: pick your 5 favorite questions, put them in your own words, and post them as Day 3. Then use one at your next consult and tell us what you heard.
