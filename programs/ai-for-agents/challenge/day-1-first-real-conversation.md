# Day 1: Your First Real Conversation

About 6 minutes · Build: your agent bio, rewritten · Post as `Day 1: my bio`

## Shot list
- On camera: open and close.
- Screen: claude.ai (or ChatGPT), fresh chat, browser at 125%.
- B-ROLL: your phone opening the app.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 1

Today you're going to have your first real conversation with AI. Not a search. A conversation.

Most agents try it once, type something like "write my bio," get something that sounds like a robot wrote it, and quit.

The problem isn't the AI. It's that it knows nothing about you. So it guesses. And guesses sound generic.

✂ CLIP START
Here's the fix. Talk to it like a brand new assistant on their first day. Tell it everything. There's even a name for why we skip this. It's the curse of knowledge. When you know something well, you forget the other person doesn't.
✂ CLIP END

**[Screen]**
Open claude.ai or ChatGPT. Either one's fine. The free version works for this whole challenge.

B-ROLL: phone opening the app.

First, the weak way. Type: "Write my real estate agent bio."

Enter.

See it? "Dedicated." "Passionate." "Your dream home." That could be anybody.

Now watch. I use six pieces every time. Who I am. What I need. Who it's for. The details. How it should sound. How long.

TEXT ON SCREEN: Who · What · For who · Details · Sound · Length

Type this. Fill in your own:

> I'm a real estate agent in [your city and area]. I've been licensed since [year]. Write a short bio for my [Facebook / Instagram / website]. It's for [who you mostly help, like first-time buyers or families moving up]. Here's what makes me different: [2 or 3 real things, like a past career, where you grew up, how you work]. Make it sound warm and plain, like I'm talking. No words like dedicated, passionate or dream home. Keep it under 80 words.

Enter.

**[Screen, reading]**
Night and day. That sounds like a person.

Now the part most people miss. It's a conversation. If something's off, just tell it.

Type: "Shorter. And lead with where I grew up."

See how it changes? You're the editor. It's the assistant.

✂ CLIP START
**[On camera]**
One rule for the whole challenge. Check everything before you use it. AI will sometimes make up details. It doesn't know it's wrong. You do.
✂ CLIP END

Your build for today: write your bio with the six pieces. Edit it until it sounds like you. Post the before and after in the group as Day 1. My before and after is pinned in the post so you can see mine first.

Tomorrow, we write follow-up texts people actually answer.
