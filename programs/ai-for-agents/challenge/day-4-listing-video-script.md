# Day 4: A Listing Video Script

About 6 minutes · Build: a 60-second listing video script · Post as `Day 4: listing script`

## Shot list
- On camera: open and close.
- Screen: the prompt.
- B-ROLL: clips from one of your listing videos (your own listing, seller-approved, or a past one).

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 4

✂ CLIP START
Most listing videos lose people in the first three seconds. They open on the front door and a song.

Nobody scrolls past a front door because it's boring. They scroll past because there's no reason to stay.

The fix is a hook. One line that makes someone want to see the next room.
✂ CLIP END

Today AI writes your next listing video script, with the hook first.

**[Screen]**
Type this. Use one of your listings or a past one:

> I'm a real estate agent in [area]. Write a 60-second talking script for a walk-through video of my listing. Here are the facts: [beds, baths, square feet, price range, 3 best features, anything special about the street or area]. Start with a one-line hook that makes someone want to keep watching, like a price point or a surprising feature. Then walk room by room in the order I'll film: [list rooms]. Use short sentences I can say out loud. End with one line telling them to message me. Mark each shot in brackets. No words like stunning, nestled or dream home.

Enter.

B-ROLL: listing video clips under the reading.

**[Screen, reading]**
Look at the hook. "Here's what $197,500 buys you in Zachary." That's the kind of line that works. My price-point tours are some of my best videos, and that's why.

Now ask for options. Type: "Give me 3 more hooks. One about the price, one about the best feature and one that's a question."

✂ CLIP START
**[On camera]**
Pick the hook that makes you want to see the house. Not the one that sounds the most professional. Curious beats polished every time.
✂ CLIP END

Check every fact against the listing before you film. AI doesn't know the house. You do.

Your build for today: one 60-second script with your favorite hook. Post it as Day 4. If you film it this week, post the video too.

Tomorrow, a week of posts in 20 minutes.

## Edit notes
- Open on the strongest listing B-roll shot, not your face, with the hook as TEXT ON SCREEN.
- Show the 3 hooks as big text cards, one after another.
- Use only listings you have the right to show.
