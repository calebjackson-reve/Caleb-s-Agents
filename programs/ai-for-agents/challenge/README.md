# 7 Days to Your First AI Assistant

The front door to AI for Agents. Every new member starts here. Modeled on the AI Automation Society 7-day challenge (see `../skool-teardown-ais-chase.md`), rebuilt for agents.

Draft for Caleb's approval.

## The promise

Start from zero. In 7 days, 10 to 20 minutes a day, you'll have an AI assistant that writes like you, plus 6 things it already did for your business.

## The days

| Day | Video | Build | Post in Skool as | From lesson |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome (90 sec) | Nothing. Say hi. | Intro post | |
| 1 | Your First Real Conversation | Your agent bio, rewritten | `Day 1: my bio` | 1 and 2 |
| 2 | The Follow-Up They Answer | 3 follow-up texts | `Day 2: follow-ups` | 3 |
| 3 | Ask Better Questions | Your 5 consult questions | `Day 3: my questions` | 4 |
| 4 | A Listing Video Script | A 60-second video script | `Day 4: listing script` | 6 |
| 5 | A Week of Posts in 20 Minutes | 5 posts drafted | `Day 5: my week` | 8 |
| 6 | What Never Goes In | Your personal AI rules | `Day 6: my rules` | 9 |
| 7 | Your Saved Assistant | A saved Claude Project or ChatGPT setup | `Day 7: done 🎓` | 10 |

## Skool setup

**Classroom course:** "7 Days to Your First AI Assistant". Unlocked for everyone. Set it to position 1, right under Start Here.
- One module per day. Video at the top, then the prompt in a copy box, then the action.
- Day 7 module includes a link to the Graduates course.

**Graduates course:** "Challenge Graduates". A single page listing everyone who posted all 7 days, updated every Friday. Shout them out on the live Q&A.

**Feed category:** "7-Day Challenge", so builds don't bury questions.

**Level reward:** finishing the challenge should land them at Level 2. Skool levels come from likes, so like every challenge post in the first month.

**Auto-DM on join (Skool plugin):**
> Hey {first name}, glad you're here. Start with Day 0 in the 7-Day Challenge, it's 90 seconds. Then tell me where you sell in the intro post. Caleb
