# Day 5: A Week of Posts in 20 Minutes

About 6 minutes · Build: 5 posts drafted · Post as `Day 5: my week`

## Shot list
- On camera: open and close.
- Screen: the prompt, then a calendar or notes app with the 5 posts.
- B-ROLL: you scrolling your feed, coffee, a drive through your area.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 5

✂ CLIP START
The hardest part of posting isn't writing. It's staring at a blank box every single day trying to think of something.

So stop deciding every day. Decide once a week.
✂ CLIP END

Today we write five posts in one sitting.

**[Screen]**
Type this:

> I'm a real estate agent in [area]. I post on [Facebook / Instagram]. My followers are mostly [people in my area, past clients, friends]. Write 5 short posts for this week, one for each type: 1) a local spot I love and why, 2) one thing buyers or sellers get wrong right now, 3) a quick story from my week (ask me for the details first), 4) a question for my followers to answer, 5) a price-point post, "here's what [price] buys in [town]". Sound like me talking. Under 80 words each. No hashtags in the text.

Enter.

**[Screen, reading]**
See number 3? It asked me for details. Good. That's the one that'll sound most like you. Answer it.

Now type: "Give me a hook for each one. The first line has to stop someone scrolling."

B-ROLL: scrolling your feed.

✂ CLIP START
**[On camera]**
Here's the psychology. When posting is a daily decision, you'll skip it. When it's already done, posting takes no willpower at all. Make the good habit the easy one.
✂ CLIP END

Your build for today: five posts, edited in your voice. Post them as Day 5. Then schedule or save them so this week is done.

Tomorrow's the most important day. What never goes into AI.
