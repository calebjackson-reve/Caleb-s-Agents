# Day 6: What Never Goes In

About 5 minutes · Build: your personal AI rules · Post as `Day 6: my rules`

## Shot list
- Mostly on camera. Serious tone, slower pace.
- Screen: the prompt and a settings menu.

## Script

**[On camera]**
TEXT ON SCREEN: Day 6

✂ CLIP START
Your clients trust you with things they don't tell anybody. Their income. Their debts. Their divorce. Their Social Security number on a pre-approval letter.

None of that goes into AI. Ever.
✂ CLIP END

This is the most important day of the challenge, so I'm going to keep it simple.

TEXT ON SCREEN: Never goes in

Never paste in:
- Social Security numbers or IDs
- Bank statements, pre-approval letters or pay stubs
- Wire or account numbers
- Full names with phone numbers, emails or addresses
- Contracts or documents with client info on them

What's fine: first names, the property details that are already public, and your own words.

✂ CLIP START
And if you ever get an email or a text asking you to change wiring instructions, stop. Call the title company on a number you already have. Wire fraud is real, and AI makes fake emails look better than ever.
✂ CLIP END

**[Screen]**
Now let's turn this into your rules. Type:

> I'm a real estate agent. Help me write a short list of personal rules for using AI in my business. Cover client privacy, checking facts before anything goes out, Fair Housing, and wire fraud. Keep it to 6 rules, one line each, in plain words.

Enter. Edit it till it's yours.

Then one setting. In ChatGPT, open Settings, then Data controls, and you can turn off "Improve the model for everyone." In Claude, check the privacy settings too. Menus change, so look for anything about training on your chats.

**[On camera]**
Your build for today: your 6 rules. Post them as Day 6. Tomorrow we put these rules inside your assistant so it follows them every time.
