Day 1 Bio Builder

AI writes a generic bio when it knows nothing about you. Tell it everything, like a brand new assistant on their first day.

The six pieces

  • Whowho you are and where you work
  • Whatwhat you need written
  • For whothe clients you help
  • Details2 or 3 real things only you have
  • Soundhow it should sound
  • Lengthhow long

The prompt. Fill in the highlighted parts.

I'm a real estate agent in [your city and area]. I've been licensed since [year]. Write a short bio for my [Facebook / Instagram / website]. It's for [the kind of clients you mostly help, like first-time buyers, move-up buyers or people selling a home they've owned for years]. Here's what makes me different: [2 or 3 real things, like a past career, where you grew up, how you work]. Make it sound warm and plain, like I'm talking. No words like dedicated, passionate or dream home. Describe what I do, not who should live where. Keep it under 80 words.

Then edit it like a conversation

  • Shorter. And lead with where I grew up.
  • Make it sound more like this text I sent a friend: [paste a text of yours, no client info]
  • Give me 3 versions. One for Instagram, one for my website, one for my email signature.

Post your build

  • Post your before and after in the 7-Day Challenge feed.
  • Title it Day 1: my bio
  • Then open Day 2 in the Classroom.
One rule for all 7 days. Check everything before you use it. AI sometimes makes up details. It doesn't know it's wrong. You do.

Fair Housing check for your bio

Your bio is advertising. Describe the service you give, not the people who should live somewhere. Cut any line that describes a person or an area by race, color, religion, sex, disability, family status or national origin, like "perfect for young families." Leave your brokerage name off until your broker's policy says it's fine. Never paste client names, numbers or details into AI.
