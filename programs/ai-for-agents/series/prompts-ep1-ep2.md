## Ep 1. You Tried It and It Was Bad

**Paste this under the video:**

> If AI gave you garbage before, it was missing three things: the job, one real detail, and your voice. Copy the prompt below, fill in the brackets, and redo the one thing that failed you.

### Prompt 1: The 3 Details

```
I need help with [THE JOB: what this is and where it goes. Example: an MLS listing description, a text to a past client, an Instagram caption].

Here's one real detail only I know: [ONE REAL DETAIL. Example: the back porch faces west, so you get the sunset].

Here's how I sound: [YOUR VOICE. Example: warm, plain, short sentences].
Never use these words: [WORDS YOU NEVER USE. Example: stunning, nestled, perfect for].

Before you write, ask me any question you need answered. Then give me one draft under [LENGTH. Example: 100 words].

Rules: describe the home, never who should live there. Don't make up facts. If you're guessing, say so.
```

### Prompt 2: Push Back

The first answer is a first draft. Paste any of these after it answers:

```
Make it shorter.
```
```
Make it sound more like me. Here's something I wrote myself: [PASTE A TEXT OR POST YOU WROTE].
```
```
Take out anything that sounds like an ad.
```
```
Give me 3 different first lines.
```

### Prompt 3: Fair Housing Check (bonus)

```
Read this listing description. Flag any word or phrase that describes who should live there instead of the home itself. Examples: families, couples, retirees, quiet, safe, walking distance to church. Rewrite it so it describes only the property.

[PASTE YOUR DESCRIPTION]
```

This helps you catch phrases you might miss. You still make the final call, and your broker has the last word.

### Your 10 minutes

Go back to the one thing AI got wrong for you. Run it again with Prompt 1, then push back once with Prompt 2.

### Keep it safe

- No client names, phone numbers, addresses or private details. Louisiana's real estate commission says personal info never goes into public AI ([LREC, Q3 2026](https://lrec.gov/blog/chairmans-corner---q3-2026)).
- Read every word before you post or send it. AI drafts, you approve.

## Ep 2. One App, Not Ten

**Paste this under the video:**

> One app on your home screen beats ten you never open. Set up ChatGPT the safe way, then ask it your first real question with the prompt below.

### The 5-step setup (checklist, not a prompt)

1. Download ChatGPT by OpenAI from the App Store or Google Play. Check that the developer says OpenAI.
2. Sign in.
3. Turn on two-factor in Settings, under Security.
4. Turn off training. In Settings, go to Data Controls and turn off "Improve the model for everyone." Menu names change, so if yours looks different, look for training or data controls.
5. Move the app to your home screen, where your thumb already goes.

Start on the free plan. Paid is about $20 a month when you're using it every day. (Price checked [DATE], recheck before posting.)

### Prompt 1: Your First Real Question

```
I'm a real estate agent in [YOUR CITY]. Before you answer, ask me 3 questions about my business. Then tell me 3 things you could help me with this week.
```

### Prompt 2: Do I Need That Other App?

```
I keep downloading AI apps. Here's what I wanted them for: [LIST WHAT EACH APP WAS SUPPOSED TO DO. Example: listing descriptions, captions, notes from calls].

For each one, tell me if you can do it yourself, and give me the exact words to ask you. Be honest if you can't do something well.
```

### Your 10 minutes

ChatGPT on your home screen, training off, Prompt 1 asked. Move the other AI apps off your home screen while you're there.

### Keep it safe

- Training off is not a free pass. Still no client names or personal info in any AI app ([LREC, Q3 2026](https://lrec.gov/blog/chairmans-corner---q3-2026)).
- Check your broker's AI policy.
