## Rules every episode follows

- 3 to 6 minutes. Full face for the hook and last line only, animation and screen for the rest (confirmed rule, 2026-10-15).
- Hook first. 80% of planning goes into the first 3 seconds (confirmed rule, 2026-10-13). Scripts will come with 2 or 3 hook options each, these are working picks.
- One psychology idea per episode (FOUNDATION value 2).
- Ends with one 10-minute action and a free download that only lives in Skool (FOUNDATION value 6, PLAN).
- Fair Housing and the LREC 3 on every episode: verify before sharing, never mislead about a property, no personal info in public AI ([LREC Chairman's Corner Q3 2026](https://lrec.gov/blog/chairmans-corner---q3-2026), confirmed).
- ChatGPT for the basics (confirmed decision 4, 2026-10-14). Phone first.

## The 10 episodes

Order follows the demand ranking, with one rule: trust comes before writing. An agent won't use AI on a listing until they know what's allowed and when it's wrong.

### Ep 1. You Tried It and It Was Bad
- **Need it answers:** #1 where do I start, #10 sounds like a robot. NAR 2026: 11% of agents say AI hurt their business, up from 4% ([Real Estate News](https://www.realestatenews.com/2026/09/22/agents-branching-out-from-generative-ai-as-adoption-grows)).
- **Hook A (recommended):** "If you tried ChatGPT once and it wrote you garbage, that wasn't you being bad at tech."
- **Hook B:** "Most agents tried AI once and quit. They all left out the same thing."
- **Outline:** why the first try felt bad (a blank page in, generic out). The fix is three real details. Before and after on screen with the same ask.
- **Psychology:** curse of knowledge. You know your listing, AI doesn't. Carries over from old Lesson 2.
- **10-minute action:** redo the one thing that failed, with three real details added.
- **Download:** the "3 details" card.

### Ep 2. One App, Not Ten
- **Need:** #5 which tool. NAR 2026: 44% say too many options. ChatGPT is used by 91% of agents who use AI.
- **Hook A (recommended):** "You don't need ten AI tools. You need one, on your home screen."
- **Hook B:** "Every 'best AI tools' video is making you slower."
- **Outline:** why the lists stall people. Pick one, why ChatGPT for now. Pin it, turn on two-factor, turn training off.
- **Psychology:** choice overload. More options, fewer decisions.
- **10-minute action:** one app on the home screen, one real question asked.
- **Download:** one-page setup checklist (same as challenge Day 1, so one file serves both).

### Ep 3. Louisiana's 3 AI Rules
- **Need:** #3 is this allowed. RPR: 49% worry about compliance, 28% about Fair Housing ([nar.realtor](https://www.nar.realtor/news/real-estate-news/technology/youve-tried-ai-but-can-you-trust-it)). Every Louisiana licensee takes the 2026 mandatory course with an AI unit by December 31.
- **Hook A (recommended):** "Louisiana's real estate commission put out three rules for AI this summer. Most agents never saw them."
- **Hook B:** "Before you paste a client's text into ChatGPT, watch this."
- **Outline:** the three LREC lines, read on screen with the link. What each looks like in a real week. "Ask your broker" step.
- **Psychology:** automation bias. A confident machine gets too much trust. Carries over from old Lesson 9.
- **10-minute action:** paste the three rules into ChatGPT's custom instructions.
- **Download:** the rules card (same file as challenge Day 2).
- **Flag:** KW First Choice AI policy answer still open (confirmed open, STATUS).

### Ep 4. Follow-Ups They Answer
- **Need:** #2 follow-up. NAR 2026: 52% of AI users write emails and follow-ups with it. Top topic across online teachers.
- **Hook A (recommended):** "'Just checking in' is the text nobody answers."
- **Hook B:** "Your follow-up isn't being ignored. It's being skipped."
- **Outline:** what makes a text easy to answer. AI drafts three in your voice. You edit and send yourself.
- **Psychology:** reciprocity and easy replies. Carries over from old Lesson 3.
- **10-minute action:** three follow-ups drafted, edited, sent by you.
- **Download:** follow-up prompt.
- **Rules:** no names or private details pasted in. No automated sending.

### Ep 5. The Listing Description That Passes Fair Housing
- **Need:** #4 listing descriptions. NAR 2026: 75% of AI users. HousingWire: AI copy "describes the people" ([May 2026](https://housingwire.com/articles/ai-fair-housing-remarks-checklist)).
- **Hook A (recommended):** "'Perfect for families.' AI writes it all the time, and it's a Fair Housing problem."
- **Hook B:** "Three words AI loves that should never be in your listing."
- **Outline:** describe the home, not the people. Before and after with the phrases flagged. A check prompt that catches them.
- **Psychology:** people buy the life they picture. Describe the moments the house makes possible, not who should live there.
- **10-minute action:** run your last listing description through the check.
- **Download:** describe-the-home check prompt plus a list of phrases to avoid.

### Ep 6. Can You Trust What It Says?
- **Need:** #6 accuracy. RPR: 63% worry about accuracy. Reddit r/realtors: co-agents' AI market stats "always end up wrong" ([thread](https://www.reddit.com/r/realtors/comments/1wdnhqj/ai_is_getting_really_weird_are_agents_actually/)).
- **Hook A (recommended):** "AI will make up a number and say it like it's a fact."
- **Hook B:** "Never ask AI for your market stats. Here's why."
- **Outline:** a live example of a confident wrong answer. The 30-second check: ask what it guessed, verify one number at the source. LREC rule 1.
- **Psychology:** the confidence heuristic. We believe whoever sounds sure.
- **10-minute action:** take one AI answer from this week and check every number.
- **Download:** the 30-second check card.

### Ep 7. Posts That Don't Sound Like AI
- **Need:** #7 social posts and #10 robot voice. NAR 2026: 56% use AI for social. Reddit and BiggerPockets: "slop," "looks fake."
- **Hook A (recommended):** "Everybody can tell your post was written by AI. Here's the tell."
- **Hook B:** "One sentence makes AI posts sound like you."
- **Outline:** why AI posts read the same (no real details). Feed it one true thing from your week. One idea, three posts.
- **Psychology:** specificity builds trust. Repetition builds familiarity. Carries over from old Lesson 8.
- **10-minute action:** one real moment from this week turned into three posts.
- **Download:** post prompt.
- **Rules:** no client names or photos without permission. Fair Housing check on every caption.

### Ep 8. The Inspection Report, in Plain English
- **Need:** #8 back office. NAR 2026: 27% use AI to summarize documents. The best Reddit answers were all back office.
- **Hook A (recommended):** "Your buyer just got a 60-page inspection report. They read page one and panicked."
- **Hook B:** "AI's best job for agents isn't writing. It's translating."
- **Outline:** practice on a sample report Caleb provides (made up, no real property). Turn it into a calm five-line text. What to say yourself.
- **Psychology:** loss aversion. A list of problems feels bigger than it is. Framing what's normal calms the decision.
- **10-minute action:** summarize the sample report, then a real one with names, addresses and prices blacked out.
- **Download:** sample report plus summary prompt.
- **Flag (inference):** check KW First Choice policy on uploading transaction documents to AI before filming.

### Ep 9. Practice the Listing Appointment
- **Need:** #8 back office (role play came up in the Reddit thread). Ties to Caleb's psychology angle.
- **Hook A (recommended):** "Practice your listing appointment with a seller who never gets tired of saying no."
- **Hook B:** "The seller's first reason for moving is almost never the real one."
- **Outline:** AI plays a seller who won't say why they're moving. You practice open questions. AI tells you what you missed.
- **Psychology:** stated reasons vs real reasons. Carries over from old Lesson 4.
- **10-minute action:** one 10-minute role play.
- **Download:** role play prompt.
- **Rules:** motivation questions only. Never ask about protected traits (Fair Housing, project rule).

### Ep 10. Your Market Update, With Your Numbers
- **Need:** #9 market reports. Real Brokerage: 34% use AI for market analysis ([PDF](https://s205.q4cdn.com/544743641/files/doc_presentation/2025/07/June-2025-Agent-Survey-Highlights-vFINAL.pdf)). Reddit: AI "still sucks for pricing."
- **Hook A (recommended):** "Don't ask AI what's happening in your market. Tell it."
- **Hook B:** "Your market update is full of numbers nobody remembers."
- **Outline:** pull your own numbers from the MLS with source and date. AI turns them into a 60-second story. Never ask it to price a home.
- **Psychology:** stories beat stats. One number with a meaning sticks.
- **10-minute action:** this month's update, from your own numbers.
- **Download:** market update prompt.
