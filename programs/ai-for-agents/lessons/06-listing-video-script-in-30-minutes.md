# Lesson 6: A Listing Video Script in 30 Minutes

Module 3: Content and Video · About 10 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Write a short listing video script with a strong hook using AI.
- **Psychology idea:** Attention. People decide in about 3 seconds whether to keep watching. Curiosity and a little story win.
- **The one win:** A 30-second script with two hook options (A and B).
- **Screen demo:** Prompt for a script built on Caleb's real example: a home that sold for more last year than it's listed for now, with upgrades. Show two hooks.
- **Show:** A clip of Caleb's golf or "documentary" video as proof that personality wins (his own content).

## Script

**[On camera]**
My best-performing video ever wasn't a fancy drone tour. It was me, being a little ridiculous, telling the story of a house that somehow hadn't sold. Over 6,000 views on TikTok, and it about doubled my followers there.

Why? Because people don't scroll for houses. They scroll for stories.

**[On camera]**
Here's the psychology. You get about three seconds. In those three seconds the brain asks one question: is this worth my time? Curiosity answers yes. A list of features answers no.

So every listing video needs a hook that opens a loop. Something they need to see the end of.

**[Screen]**
Here's my prompt:
"I'm a real estate agent in Louisiana. Write a 30-second vertical video script for a listing. The story: the owners bought it last year for $352,000, put $40,000 into upgrades, and now it's listed at $344,900 on a golf course. Give me 2 different hook options for the first 3 seconds that create curiosity. Then the script with what I say and what's on screen. End by asking viewers to message me a keyword. Sound casual and a little funny, like a real person."

**[Screen, reading]**
Hook A: "The only mystery here is how this house hasn't sold."
Hook B: "They lost money on this house so you don't have to."

Two totally different angles. Film both. Post one and save the other, or test them.

**[On camera]**
Two rules. First, every number you say must be true and current, so check it against the MLS before you film. Second, make it sound like you. If you'd never say a line out loud, cut it.

Your one action: write a 30-second script for one listing, or a past one, with two hooks. Post your favorite hook in the Skool group and we'll vote.
