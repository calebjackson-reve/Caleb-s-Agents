# Lesson 1: You're Not Behind

Module 1: The Basics · About 8 minutes · Talking to camera plus screen share

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Get a nervous agent to open an AI tool and ask a first question today.
- **Psychology idea:** Status quo bias. Our brains prefer the familiar way, even when the new way is easier. Fear of looking dumb keeps people stuck.
- **The one win:** Create a free account in Claude or ChatGPT and ask one real question about their business.
- **Screen demo:** claude.ai sign-up page, then the chat box. Ask: "I'm a real estate agent in Baton Rouge. What are 5 ways I could use you this week?"
- **Notes:** Show the free sign-up only. Don't quote prices. Keep the camera tight and friendly.

## Script

**[On camera]**
Most agents I talk to say the same thing about AI. "I know I should be using it. I just don't know where to start." And then they feel behind.

You're not behind. I promise. Most agents haven't touched it yet. If you start today, you're ahead of most of your market by Friday.

I'm Caleb Jackson. I sell real estate in Louisiana with Keller Williams First Choice, and I've spent the last year using AI every single day in my business. This series starts at the very beginning. No tech words. No ten-step systems. One small win per video.

**[On camera]**
Quick bit of psychology first, because it explains why this feels hard.

Your brain likes what it already knows. Even when the old way takes longer, the new way feels risky. Psychologists call it status quo bias. Add the fear of looking dumb, and most people just don't start.

So here's the deal. Today you're not learning AI. You're asking it one question. That's it.

**[Screen]**
Go to claude.ai. You can also use ChatGPT. They work the same way for what we're doing. I'll use Claude.

Click sign up. Use your email or Google account. It's free to start.

Now you see one box. That's the whole tool. You type like you're texting a really smart assistant.

I'm going to type this, word for word:
"I'm a real estate agent in Baton Rouge. What are 5 ways I could use you this week to save time?"

Hit enter.

**[Screen, reading the answer]**
Look at that. Follow-up texts. Listing descriptions. Social posts. Prepping for a listing appointment. It just gave me a to-do list for my week.

**[On camera]**
That's it. You used AI. The scariest part is over.

Here's your one action. Open Claude or ChatGPT today, type that same question with your own city, and read the answer. Then come post your favorite idea in the Wins section of my free Skool group.

The full beginner path is in there, in order, and every Friday I go live to answer questions. Link's in the description.

See you there.
