# Lesson 4: The Real Reason They're Moving

Module 2: Talking to Clients · About 10 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Teach agents to find the real motivation behind a move, and practice it with AI.
- **Psychology idea:** Stated reasons vs real reasons. People give the safe, surface answer first. The real reason comes out with open questions, patience and trust.
- **The one win:** Run one AI role-play of a seller conversation and write down three questions that worked.
- **Screen demo:** Ask AI to play a seller who says "we just want more space" but has a hidden reason. Practice uncovering it.
- **Fair Housing note on screen:** Keep questions about timeline, money, goals and the move. Never probe or steer on family status, religion, race, disability or other protected traits.

## Script

**[On camera]**
When a seller says "we just want more space," that's almost never the whole story.

Sometimes it's a new job. Sometimes money's tight. Sometimes it's a life change they're not ready to say out loud. People give you the safe answer first. The real reason comes later, if you earn it.

Why does this matter? Because the real reason changes everything. Your pricing. Your timeline. How hard they'll negotiate. Whether they'll actually sign.

**[On camera]**
Here's the psychology. People protect themselves. A stranger asks a personal question, and the brain gives a clean, simple answer. To get past that, you need three things. Open questions. Patience with silence. And curiosity instead of pressure.

Open questions start with what, how, or tell me. "What's got you thinking about moving now?" lands way better than "Why are you selling?"

And here's where AI comes in. You can practice this before a real appointment.

**[Screen]**
I'll type:
"Play the role of a home seller in Louisiana. You tell me you want to sell because you want more space, but there's a different real reason you're not sharing yet. Only reveal it if I ask good open questions and make you feel comfortable. Don't tell me the reason up front. Keep your answers short and realistic. I'm the agent. Start when I say hello."

Then I just talk to it like a real seller.

**[Screen, role-play]**
Me: "Hey, thanks for having me. What's got you thinking about a move right now?"
AI: "Oh, we just need more room."
Me: "Totally get it. If everything went perfectly, where would you be living a year from now?"

Watch how it opens up when the questions get softer and more curious.

**[Screen]**
When you're done, ask it: "What did I do well, and which question should I have asked?" Free coaching.

**[On camera]**
One line we never cross. Keep questions on the move itself. Timeline, money, goals, what they want next. Never dig into or steer based on family status, religion, disability or anything like that. That's Fair Housing, and it's also just respect.
