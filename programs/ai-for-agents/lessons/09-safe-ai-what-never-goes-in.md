# Lesson 9: Safe AI, What Never Goes In

Module 4: Mindset and Safety · About 8 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Give agents simple, memorable rules for using AI safely.
- **Psychology idea:** Automation bias. People trust a confident answer from a machine too much. AI sounds sure even when it's wrong.
- **The one win:** A personal "AI rules" card saved on their phone.
- **Screen demo:** Show AI confidently giving a wrong or unverifiable fact, then show how to check it.
- **Rules on screen:** Never put in client financial info, Social Security numbers, bank or wire details, or passwords. Check every fact. Follow your brokerage's tech policy. Fair Housing applies to AI writing too. You approve everything before it goes out.

## Script

**[On camera]**
AI is incredibly useful. It's also confidently wrong sometimes.

There's a name for trusting machines too much. Automation bias. When something sounds sure of itself, we believe it. AI always sounds sure of itself.

So today, five rules. Screenshot them.

**[On camera]**
Rule one. Never put sensitive client info in. No Social Security numbers, no bank details, no loan documents, no passwords. You don't need them to get great drafts.

Rule two. Wire instructions never touch AI. Wire fraud is one of the biggest risks in our business. Keep those conversations on your brokerage and title company's secure process.

Rule three. Check every fact. Numbers, dates, laws, school info, tax rules. If you didn't verify it, don't post it.

**[Screen]**
Watch. I'll ask: "What's the average days on market in Zachary, Louisiana right now?"

It gives me a number. Sounds great. But where'd it come from? I have no idea. So I check the MLS. That's my source, not the chatbot.

**[On camera]**
Rule four. Fair Housing applies to AI too. If AI writes "perfect for young families" or describes who a neighborhood is "right for," fix it. Describe the home, not the people.

Rule five. You approve everything. AI drafts. You decide. Your name is on it.

Also check your brokerage's policy on AI tools. Some have rules about which ones you can use.

Your one action: write your own AI rules card in your phone's notes with these five rules. Post "done" in the Skool group.
