# Lesson 10: Your First Saved Assistant

Module 5: Your First Assistant · About 11 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Set up AI so it remembers who they are and how they sound, so every answer starts better.
- **Psychology idea:** Habit design. Make the good behavior easy. When the setup is done once, using it daily takes no willpower.
- **The one win:** A saved setup (a Claude Project or ChatGPT custom instructions) with their business details and voice.
- **Screen demo:** Create a Claude Project called "My Real Estate Assistant." Add instructions: who you are, market, brokerage, voice, rules from Lesson 9. Test with a follow-up text request.
- **Bridge:** Tease the paid tier or one-on-one sessions, where Caleb shows his full system.

## Script

**[On camera]**
For nine lessons you've been typing who you are into every prompt. "I'm a real estate agent in Louisiana..." over and over.

Today we fix that. One setup, and AI remembers you every time.

**[On camera]**
Quick psychology. Willpower runs out. Good habits stick when they're easy. The best way to use AI every day is to make it so easy you don't have to think about it.

**[Screen]**
In Claude, click Projects, then New Project. Name it "My Real Estate Assistant." If you use ChatGPT, look for custom instructions in settings. Same idea.

Now the instructions. I'll show mine, and you'll write yours:

"I'm a REALTOR in the Baton Rouge area with Keller Williams First Choice. I work with buyers and sellers. My voice is warm, direct and natural. Keep things short. No fancy words like stunning or nestled. Never include client personal or financial information. Describe homes, never people, to follow Fair Housing. Always remind me to verify numbers before posting."

Save.

**[Screen]**
Now watch. Inside the project I just type:
"Follow-up text for an open house visitor."

That's it. It already knows my market, my voice and my rules.

**[On camera]**
You just built your first AI assistant. Seriously. That's what all the fancy tools are doing under the hood.

This wraps the beginner path. You went from never opening AI to having your own assistant that knows your business.

What's next? I use a much bigger version of this in my business every day, with assistants that review my videos and plan my week. In my one-on-one sessions I help agents build theirs. Details are in the Skool group.

Your one action: set up your assistant today and post a screenshot of your instructions in the group, with any personal details blurred.

I'm proud of you for finishing. See you Friday on the live call.
