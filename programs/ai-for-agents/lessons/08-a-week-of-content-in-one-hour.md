# Lesson 8: A Week of Content in One Hour

Module 3: Content and Video · About 9 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Turn one idea into a week of posts across platforms.
- **Psychology idea:** Repetition builds trust. People need to see you many times before they reach out. One idea said five ways beats five random ideas.
- **The one win:** Five posts drafted from one idea, edited in their own voice.
- **Screen demo:** Take one topic ("what a buyer should do the week before closing") and ask for a Facebook post, an Instagram caption, a 20-second video script, a text to past clients and a question post.

## Script

**[On camera]**
Most agents think they need a new idea every day. That's why they stop posting.

You don't need more ideas. You need one good idea, said five different ways.

Here's the psychology. People need to see you again and again before they trust you enough to call. Marketers have said for years it takes many touches. And repeating one clear message makes you the person people think of for that thing.

**[Screen]**
Let's turn one idea into a week.
"I'm a real estate agent in Louisiana. My topic this week is: 3 things buyers should do the week before closing. Write: 1) a Facebook post, 2) an Instagram caption, 3) a 20-second video script with a hook, 4) a short text I could send to past clients who might know a buyer, 5) a question post that gets comments. Same idea, different format for each. Friendly, simple, no hashtags except on Instagram."

**[Screen, scrolling]**
Five pieces. One idea. Took about a minute.

Now I spend the rest of my hour making them sound like me and filming the 20-second video.

**[On camera]**
A quick tip. Keep a running list of questions clients ask you. Every question is a week of content. "Can I paint before closing?" "Do I need a survey?" Those are gold.

Your one action: pick one question a client asked you this month, turn it into five posts with AI, and schedule or post them this week. Share your favorite in the Skool group.
