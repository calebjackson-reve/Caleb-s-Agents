# Lesson 3: Follow-Ups That Get Answered

Module 1: The Basics · About 9 minutes

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Use AI to draft short, human follow-up texts for leads and past clients.
- **Psychology idea:** Reciprocity and easy replies. People respond when you give something first and when answering takes two seconds.
- **The one win:** Three follow-up texts ready to send, edited in their own voice.
- **Screen demo:** Prompt for an open house visitor follow-up, a cold online lead and a past client check-in.
- **Rule on screen:** AI drafts. You read, edit and send. No client personal info in the prompt.

## Script

**[On camera]**
Most follow-up texts don't get answered for one reason. They're about us. "Just checking in!" "Let me know if you have any questions!"

People reply when two things are true. You gave them something first. And replying is easy.

That's reciprocity. Give before you ask. And make the reply a one-word answer.

**[Screen]**
Let's build it. I'll use the six-piece prompt from last lesson.

"I'm a real estate agent in Louisiana. Write 3 short follow-up texts for someone who visited my open house on Saturday. Each text should give them something useful first, like a nearby sale or a quick tip, then ask a question they can answer in one word. Sound like a friendly local person, not a salesman. Under 40 words each. No emojis."

Enter.

**[Screen, reading one out]**
"Hey, thanks for stopping by Saturday. A similar home two streets over just sold in 9 days, so this one may move fast. Still on your list, yes or no?"

That's a text I'd actually reply to.

**[Screen]**
Now a past client, the people most agents forget:
"Write a check-in text for a past client who bought a home 2 years ago. Give them one useful thing, like a reminder to check their homestead exemption or HVAC filter. No sales pitch. Under 35 words."

**[On camera]**
Notice what I didn't put in there. Names, phone numbers, addresses of my clients. You don't need them. You add the name when you send it.

And always read it before you send. Change any word that doesn't sound like you. AI is your first draft, not your voice.

Your one action: write three follow-up texts with AI today and send one. Then tell us in the Skool group if you got a reply.

That wraps the basics. Next module, we get into my favorite part. Psychology. How to find the real reason someone wants to sell.
