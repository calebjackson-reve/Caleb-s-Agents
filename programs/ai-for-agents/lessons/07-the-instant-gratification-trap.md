# Lesson 7: The Instant Gratification Trap

Module 4: Mindset and Safety · About 10 minutes · Mostly on camera

## Teaching plan

- **Goal:** Help people use AI to buy back time for what matters, not to scroll more.
- **Psychology idea:** Instant gratification. Our brains chase quick rewards (likes, notifications, scrolling) over bigger, slower rewards. AI can feed that loop or break it.
- **The one win:** A one-day time audit, and one hour reclaimed using AI.
- **Screen demo:** Ask AI to sort a list of weekly tasks into "AI can draft," "only I can do," and "stop doing."
- **Care note:** This is perspective and habits, not therapy. If someone mentions depression or feeling hopeless, encourage professional help, including the 988 Suicide and Crisis Lifeline (call or text 988 in the US).

## Script

**[On camera]**
I want to talk about something bigger than real estate for a minute.

We live in an instant world. Food in 20 minutes. Answers in 2 seconds. Likes every time we post. And our brains love it. Every quick reward feels good, so we keep chasing the next one.

The problem is that the things that matter most are slow. Building a business. Relationships. Health. Peace of mind. They don't give you a hit of anything today.

**[On camera]**
Here's my honest take on AI. It can make this worse, or it can make it a lot better.

If you use it to make more content to scroll, more noise, more busy work, you'll just run faster in the same circle. But if you use it to hand off the busy work, you get time back. And what you do with that time is the real win.

**[Screen]**
Let's do this practically. I'll list my week.
"Here are my weekly tasks: writing listing descriptions, follow-up texts, scheduling showings, social media captions, prepping CMAs, calling past clients, client meetings, paperwork reminders, scrolling social media for ideas. Sort them into three groups: AI can draft it, only I can do it, and I should stop or cut back. Explain each in one line."

**[Screen, reading]**
AI can draft: descriptions, captions, follow-up texts, reminders.
Only I can do: client meetings, calling past clients.
Cut back: scrolling for ideas.

That's easily a few hours a week, back.

**[On camera]**
Now the important part. Decide right now where that time goes. More calls to past clients? Dinner with your family? A walk with no phone? Pick it before the time shows up, or the scroll will take it.

And one real thing. If you're dealing with more than a busy week, if you're feeling down or hopeless for a while, please talk to someone. A doctor, a counselor, or in the US you can call or text 988 any time. That's strength, not weakness.

Your one action: track one day of your time. Then use AI to hand off one task and win back one hour. Tell us in the group what you did with it.
