# Production Playbook: When to Film, What to Film, How to Edit

Draft for Caleb. Built around the 12-hour week in `PLAN.md`.

## What to film

| Type | Length | How often | Where it goes | Script source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Challenge videos** | 4 to 7 min | Once, 8 videos | Skool classroom, unlisted on YouTube | `challenge/` |
| **Long lesson** | 8 to 12 min | 1 a week | YouTube, then Skool | `lessons/01` to `10` |
| **Short clips** | 30 to 60 sec | 3 to 5 per lesson | Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube Shorts | Marked `✂ CLIP` in each script |

## When to film

**Batch day 1, week 2: the challenge (about 4 hours).** Film all 8 challenge videos in one session. Same shirt and same setup so they match.
- 0:00 to 0:30, setup and test recording
- 0:30 to 2:00, on-camera parts of all 8 videos, in order
- 2:00 to 2:15, break
- 2:15 to 4:00, all screen recordings, in order

**Every Tuesday: the weekly lesson (2 hours).**
- 30 min setup and a read-through out loud
- 45 min on camera
- 45 min screen recording

**Film on-camera first, screen second.** Your energy is highest at the start. Screen recordings can be redone any time.

**Best time of day:** whenever you have steady light and a quiet house. Pick one slot and keep it every week so the lighting matches.

**Filming order for weeks 1 to 12**

| Week | Film | Publish |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Test setup. Film Lesson 1. | Nothing yet |
| 2 | Challenge batch day (8 videos). Lessons 2 and 3. | Nothing yet |
| 3 | Lesson 4 | Challenge goes live, Lessons 1 to 3, launch post |
| 4 to 10 | Lessons 5 to 10, one a week | The lesson filmed the week before |
| 11 to 12 | 2 lessons from member questions | Same |

Always keep one lesson filmed ahead. If you miss a Tuesday, nothing slips.

## The setup (one-time)

You likely have most of this already. Approve any purchase first.
- **Camera:** your iPhone, rear camera, 4K at 30fps, on a tripod at eye level. Lock exposure: tap and hold on your face.
- **Mic:** a clip-on wireless mic (a DJI Mic or Rode Wireless GO class). Audio matters more than video.
- **Light:** face a window, or one soft light at 45 degrees in front of you. Never a window behind you.
- **Background:** the same spot every time. A real room, a little depth, nothing messy. No brokerage signage unless KW policy allows it.
- **Screen recording:** Mac built-in (Shift+Command+5), full screen, or Loom. Zoom the browser to 125% so text is readable on a phone. Close every tab, email and notification first. **No client names or info on screen, ever.**
- **Teleprompter:** a free phone teleprompter app, or read one line, look up and say it. Don't read word for word. Scripts are a guide. Sounding like you beats perfect lines.

## How to read the scripts

- **[On camera]** is you talking to the lens.
- **[Screen]** is a screen recording with your voice over it.
- **✂ CLIP** marks a 30 to 60 second section that works as a stand-alone short.
- **B-ROLL** marks a spot for a quick cutaway: a listing photo, you texting, a street in Zachary.
- **TEXT ON SCREEN** is a caption or graphic to add in editing.

## How to edit (CapCut desktop, step by step)

CapCut is free and handles everything here. Descript works too if you'd rather edit by deleting words from a transcript.

**1. Set up the project (2 min)**
- New project, 16:9, 1080p or 4K, 30fps.
- Make folders: A-roll (camera), Screen, B-roll, Music.

**2. Rough cut (30 to 45 min for a 10-minute lesson)**
- Drop all camera clips in order.
- Cut every mistake, restart and long pause. When you flubbed a line, keep the last good take only.
- Cut the breath and "um" at the start of each sentence. Leave natural pauses under half a second.
- Insert the screen recordings where the script says [Screen].
- Target length: script length plus 10%. If a lesson runs over 12 minutes, cut the second example, not the hook.

**3. The first 30 seconds (spend extra time here)**
- Start on your first spoken line. No logo, no intro music, no "hey guys."
- Add a text hook at 0:00 matching the title (TEXT ON SCREEN in the script).
- Cut to the screen or B-roll by 0:20 so the picture changes.

**4. Keep it moving**
- Change the picture every 5 to 10 seconds: a punch-in (scale camera to 115%), a screen cut, a B-roll or a text callout.
- On screen recordings, zoom in on what you're clicking (CapCut: keyframe scale to 150 to 200% on the prompt box). Nobody can read a full browser on a phone.
- Highlight the prompt text with a box or underline when you read it.

**5. Captions**
- Auto captions (Captions, then Auto captions). Proofread every one. AI captions get names and street names wrong.
- Long videos: one clean style, bottom third, white text with a dark shadow.
- Short clips: bigger, centered, 2 to 4 words at a time, keyword in a single accent color.

**6. Audio**
- Normalize loudness (Audio, Normalize, around -14 LUFS).
- Turn on noise reduction if there's hum.
- Music optional, under 10% volume, and only CapCut's licensed library or YouTube's Audio Library. Drop it to zero while you're explaining a prompt.

**7. The end**
- Last 15 seconds: your one action, then "The template's free in my Skool group, link in the description."
- Add a TEXT ON SCREEN of the Skool link.
- No long outro. End within 3 seconds of your last word.

**8. Export**
- 1080p, 30fps, high bitrate. Name it `L03-follow-ups-FINAL.mp4`.

**9. Thumbnail (YouTube only)**
- Your face, real expression, big. 3 to 5 words max. One bright color behind the text.
- Show the before and after when you can (a bad text vs a good one).

## Cutting short clips (30 min per lesson)

1. Find each ✂ CLIP section in the script.
2. Duplicate the project, set it to 9:16.
3. Reframe so your face sits in the top half, and put screen shots in the bottom half when needed.
4. The first spoken line must work with no context. If it starts with "So," or "And," cut that word.
5. Big captions, as above.
6. End with "Full lesson's free in my Skool, link in my profile." Don't say "link below." Each app is different.

## Upload checklist

**YouTube**
- Title: the promise in plain words, under 60 characters. Example: "AI Follow-Up Texts That Actually Get Answers (For Agents)"
- Description, first line: "Free template and the 7-day challenge: [Skool link]"
- Chapters: timestamps for each section.
- Pinned comment: the Skool link plus the one action.

**Skool**
- Post in the classroom under the right module.
- Post in the feed: "🎥 New lesson: [title]. Your one action: [action]. Post it below when you've done it."
- Attach the template or prompt as a file or text in the lesson.

**Clips**
- Draft in Metricool for Facebook, Instagram and TikTok. Caleb approves before scheduling.
