## Join questions (Skool allows up to 3, historical)

1. Are you a licensed real estate agent? What state?
2. How many unread texts are on your phone right now? Just the number.
3. Have you used ChatGPT or any AI before? Never, a little, or every week.

Question 2 doubles as the Day 0 hook. It gets them looking at their phone before they're even in.

## Rules

1. **Be kind.** Everybody here starts at step 1.
2. **No client names, texts or screenshots.** Share numbers and wins, never people.
3. **No money details in AI.** Loan amounts, account numbers and health info never go in.
4. **Fair Housing, always.** Sort people by how you know them, never by who they are.
5. **No selling or recruiting.** No lead offers, no brokerage pitches, no DMs pitching members.
6. **Post your Day.** Watching isn't learning. Show us what you did.
