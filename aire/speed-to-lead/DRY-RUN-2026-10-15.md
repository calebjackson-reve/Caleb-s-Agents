## Compliance check (ce-compliant-outbound-guardrails)
- These are inbound inquiries, so replying by email and text is consent-based, not cold outreach.
- Sender identity and brokerage are in every message. Opt-out line is on the text.
- No price, value or timing claims. "Real comps, not the automated number" is a service statement.
- Texting a consumer number requires a registered business line (A2P 10DLC). A personal cell through an app is not that.
