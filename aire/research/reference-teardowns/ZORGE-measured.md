## Scroll model
- Virtual scroll on desktop: wrapper `page-content js-page-content` 36633 px tall, body overflow hidden. Wheel input is ignored. Keyboard drives it after the intro arms Locomotive Scroll. The desktop full page is a keyboard-driven stitch of 20 slices covering the first 18,000 px of 36,633 (about half), stopped at the pinned sections. The mobile full page is complete at 25,669 px. Section tops and heights for the whole desktop page are in the table above from the live transforms, so the type and layout evidence is complete even where the stitched image is not.
