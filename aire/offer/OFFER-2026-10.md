# The agent offer, October 2026 draft

Built with `agent-offer-builder` and `ce-offer-formula`. Nothing here is posted, priced or sold until Caleb says so. Prices are recommendations, labeled inference.

## The system in one sentence
The AI office: a Monday brief of what changed in AI, one memory file of which tool does what, a compliance check on every outbound draft, and one approvals page with a button per decision, so an agent runs their business with AI in under an hour a week of their own time.

## The gate
The offer skill's rule: Caleb sells what he uses, and only after it has run for real twice. As of October 19, 2026 the brief has run once, the compliance check once, the approvals page is one day old. So the free tier can launch now because it teaches rather than promises. The workshop and the done-for-you tier stay drafts until the second real week (October 26) is in the log.

## Tier 1, free, Skool
A post plus one short lesson. The post is drafted at `programs/ai-for-agents/posts/ai-office-in-a-day.md`.
- What the agent gets: the idea, the first step (the stack memory file), and the one-page template.
- What it costs Caleb: 20 minutes to review and post, 30 minutes a week answering replies. Inference.
- Offer sentence: "Post your current tool list and I'll hand you back a one-page stack memory for your office within 48 hours, free, no strings."
- Friction: one comment. No call.

## Tier 2, workshop, 90 minutes live
Under `programs/ai-for-agents`, replacing the retired ai-workshops draft. "Set up your AI office in 90 minutes."
Outline:
1. 0 to 10: why the floor is rising (the Realtor.com and Zillow agent-side AI, from the October brief).
2. 10 to 30: the stack memory. Each agent fills their own STACK.md live from the template.
3. 30 to 55: speed to lead. Each agent writes their two templates (daytime, after hours) and runs the compliance checklist on them by hand.
4. 55 to 75: the weekly brief. Each agent installs the caleb-skills plugin or uploads the chat zip and runs their first brief.
5. 75 to 90: the approvals page idea, Q and A.
- What they leave with: a filled STACK.md, two compliant templates, the brief running, the checklist.
- What it costs Caleb: 3 hours prep the first time, 90 minutes delivery, 1 hour follow-up per cohort. Inference.
- Subscriptions the agent needs: Claude Pro or Max, Google Workspace or equivalent. Nothing paid beyond what they likely have.
- Offer sentence: "In 90 minutes you leave with your own AI office running: stack memory, two compliant lead replies, and a Monday brief that writes itself. If it isn't running when we hang up, you don't pay."
- Price recommendation: $149 a seat, cohorts of 8 to 12. Inference from the Skool hub's $27 to $49 paid tier plan and a 3 to 5 month equivalence. Caleb sets the price.
- What breaks if their state differs: the compliance checklist is Louisiana. Other states' advertising rules are unknown. The workshop says so and has the agent write their state's rule in the top line of their own checklist.

## Tier 3, done-for-you install
Caleb (or Claude under Caleb) installs the caleb-skills plugin on the agent's machine with a STACK.md tuned to their brokerage, CRM and state, plus the approvals page pointed at their accounts.
- Gets installed: the plugin, STACK.md, the four AIRE skills, the weekly brief schedule, the net sheet calculator with their defaults, the compliance checklist with a state rules intake filled in.
- Stays Caleb's: the Nick Saraev course skills stay in the repo as is (his free course, attributed), the speed to lead runner (needs their own Gmail and CRM wiring, scoped separately), AIRE itself.
- State rules intake: before install, the agent answers five questions: state, brokerage name and license number format, required disclosures on advertising, texting consent rules they follow, whether their brokerage has an AI policy. Unknown answers become "ask your broker" lines in their checklist.
- What it costs Caleb: 4 hours per install the first three times, then about 2. Plus a 30 minute check-in at 30 days. Inference.
- Subscriptions the agent needs: Claude Max (for the API credits and the sidebar), Google Workspace.
- What breaks if their CRM differs: KW Command is browser-only. Follow Up Boss or kvCORE have APIs, which is easier, but the enrichment and speed to lead directives would need their export format. Scope that as a second engagement.
- Offer sentence: "I'll install the whole AI office on your machine in one sitting, tuned to your brokerage and your state, and check in at 30 days. If it isn't saving you an hour a week by then, I refund the install."
- Price recommendation: $497 install plus the agent's own subscriptions. Inference. Caleb sets the price.
