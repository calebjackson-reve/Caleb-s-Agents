How I work

Your call gets returned today. Not tomorrow.

01020304

  1. Read the house honestly

    What it does well, what a buyer will question, and what's worth fixing before it goes live. You get the straight read, not the flattering one.

  2. Decide how it's seen

    Photography, the order of rooms and the first line of the listing, chosen on purpose. Basil Lane above is how that looks in practice.

  3. Price straight, negotiate hard

    A number backed by the comps and the condition, then a plan for every offer that comes in. No guessing, no "let's see."

    You hear from me directly, with plain options at every decision through closing. Most of my business comes from people I've already closed for, and the people they send me.
