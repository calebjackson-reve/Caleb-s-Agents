Before we talk

A few straight answers.

Can I use the tools without contacting you?

Yes. Results and checklists are free and available immediately. Your calculator entries stay in the page. No email or account is required.

Are the budget results lender quotes?

No. They use editable examples. Replace the assumptions with address-specific taxes, insurance and lender figures before relying on them.

Can you help if I’m still deciding whether to move?

Start with the buyer or seller planning path. If you want to talk through your priorities after that, text or call Caleb.

Does a home near a golf course include golf access?

Do not assume it does. Club membership, access, dues and property restrictions need separate confirmation with the club and the property documents.

What should I send in my first text?

A home address or area, your approximate timing, and the question you need answered is a useful start. You do not need to have the whole plan figured out.
