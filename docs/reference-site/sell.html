Before you list

A few straight answers.

Is this a valuation of my home?

No. It is arithmetic on the numbers you enter. A pricing opinion needs the home itself, recent comparable sales and a conversation.

What usually comes out of a sale price?

Your loan payoff, agreed compensation to brokers, title and settlement charges, any credits you agree to, and prorated items such as property taxes. Your title company’s estimated settlement statement is the document to check.

Are commission rates set?

No. Compensation is negotiable and agreed in writing. The planner uses the percentage you enter.

Should I fix things before listing?

Sometimes. Start with safety items and anything an inspector or a buyer’s lender is likely to flag, and get prices before you commit. The preparation budget keeps that cost visible.

Will my home get its own page like Basil Lane?

That depends on the home, the timing and the budget. Basil Lane is one example. Ask me what I would recommend for yours.
