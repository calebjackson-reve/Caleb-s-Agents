## 2. What you're selling

**Promise:** In 30 days, a working agent sets up AI tools that save them 5+ hours a week and produce better marketing than their competition.

**Who it's for:** Solo agents and small teams, mostly 1 to 10 years in, who know AI matters but don't know where to start. They don't want to code.

**What they get:**
- Plug-and-play AI workflows for listings, follow-up, content and market data
- Real examples from a working agent in Louisiana, not theory
- Early access to AIRE

**Offer ladder (start free, add paid later):**
1. Free community: weekly tips, first 3 workflows, Q&A
2. Paid tier later (decide after 50 to 100 members): full course, templates, live calls
3. AIRE software: the product the community feeds into

## 3. Core values

1. **Real work over hype.** Every lesson is something Caleb actually uses on real deals.
2. **Time back is the scoreboard.** If a tool doesn't save time or win business, it's out.
3. **Agents stay human.** AI does the busywork. Relationships stay with the agent.
4. **Do it right.** Follow MLS rules, fair housing, privacy and brokerage policy. No shortcuts that put a license at risk.
5. **Show the receipts.** Share real results, wins and misses.

## 6. First 5 videos to film

Keep each one 5 to 12 minutes. Film on your phone or a webcam with a clean background. Talk like you're explaining it to an agent friend.

1. **Welcome (2 to 3 min):** who you are, why this exists, what members get, first step to take.
2. **My AI stack as a working agent:** the exact tools you use and what each does.
3. **Write a listing description that doesn't sound like AI:** live demo with Claude and Humanizer.
4. **Find the best deals in your market:** walk through the Deal Finder with an MLS export.
5. **The 10-minute follow-up system:** AI-drafted follow-ups you review and send yourself.

**Simple script shape for each:**
- Hook (15 sec): the problem in one sentence
- Show it (most of the video): screen recording, real example
- Result: what it saved or produced
- Next step: one action for members to try today
