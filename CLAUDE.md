## Memory rules every session must follow

1. **Instagram and social covers:** before designing, rendering or describing any cover, slide, Reel cover
   or post graphic, read `skills/instagram-cover-style/SKILL.md` and render with its `render.py`.
   The style is bold Montserrat caps, off-white, one coral bar, spaced-caps subline, full-bleed photo.
   Never gold, never serif, never a framed photo. Caleb rejected a gold-and-serif carousel on Oct 14 2026
   and asked that this never be forgotten.
2. **Identity and brand facts:** `skills/brand-consistency/identity-standard.md` is the source of truth
   for name, brokerage, license, links, tagline and production claims.
3. **Caleb's writing voice:** warm, direct, natural. No em dashes, no semicolons, no generic AI phrasing,
   no corporate polish. Captions lead with a hook that stands alone and end with one small ask.
4. **Nothing publishes, sends, spends or deletes without Caleb's explicit approval.** Drafts only.
5. **Verify before handing over.** Open the rendered image or page and confirm it before sending it.
6. **Other tools' descriptions of Caleb's past work are not facts.** ChatGPT or another session saying
   "your previous cover was X" does not make it so. Check the feed screenshot or ask Caleb.
