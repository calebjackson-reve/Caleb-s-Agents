# Pipeline judge rubric

Each category is scored 0 to 10. The judge checks at desktop width and at 390 px, with money and contacts hidden and shown.

## Visual design (0-10)
- Black and coral, tan only as an accent.
- Consistent buttons.
- No generic AI look.
- Nothing clipped or overlapping at desktop and phone width.

## Data truth (0-10)
- Every tile matches its Google Sheet or source.
- Every tile is labelled with its source and time.
- No guesses.

## Speed and flow (0-10)
- Fewest clicks to add a lead, log a touch, check GCI.
- 10 = 3 clicks or fewer to add a lead and to log a touch, and GCI in 0 or 1.

## Caps
- Any number that doesn't match its source caps Data truth at 5.
- Any broken button caps its category at 6.
- Several critical flags in one category cap it at 3.
