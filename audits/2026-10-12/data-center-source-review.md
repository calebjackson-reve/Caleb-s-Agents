## Reconciled issues

- The project is Hut 8 River Bend in West Feliciana. Do not confuse it with Meta's Richland Parish campus.
- Larger annual revenue figures in September news are forecasts. Article uses the parish's assessment-dependent description rather than guaranteeing an exact annual payout.
- Jobs vary by definition and phase. Temporary construction jobs, direct operations jobs and induced jobs cannot be added or substituted casually.
- Cooling design is not a complete water-use assessment. Official agreement includes a well. No zero-water or no-groundwater guarantee.
- Announced investment is capital spending, not a town cash transfer. Advance payments must be treated together with reimbursement terms.
- Road, school, public service, workforce and retail scenarios in the article are Caleb's selected optimistic opinion. No new restaurant, hotel, tax credit, school program or particular road construction is asserted as approved.
- Real estate demand effects are inferred possibilities. No measured parish price increase or guaranteed appreciation was found or claimed.

## Acceptance and limits

Finished article links its central primary sources at the claims. Source-reported plans and opinion have separate sections. Date, geographic distinction and limitations remain visible. Complete contract interpretation, environmental engineering review, public-budget reconciliation and exhaustive source coverage remain outside this content review.
