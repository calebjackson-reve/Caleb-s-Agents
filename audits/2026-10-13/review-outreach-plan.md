# Review outreach plan

Date: October 13, 2026. Owner: Caleb. Goal: honest public reviews on Google first, Zillow second, from real clients, one at a time.

## Rules we follow
- Ask real clients only, after a closed deal or a clearly finished step.
- No incentives, no gift cards, no "leave us 5 stars." Ask for an honest review of what the process was like.
- Never ask only the happy ones (Google calls that gating). Ask everyone we closed.
- One personal text or call per person, from Caleb's phone, not a blast.
- Space Google and Zillow asks at least a week apart for the same person.
- Reply to every review within a day in Caleb's voice.
- Keep the tracker private (phone numbers, outcomes). Only counts go in this repo.
