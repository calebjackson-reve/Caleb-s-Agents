# Outcome policy cache

- Scope: Daily Five ranking only.
- Evidence: current leaf `reply` and `no_reply` labels on accepted surfacings.
- Minimum sample: 30 mature outcomes globally and 10 for the exact reason.
- Adjustment: at most five percent up or down from the fixed reason weight.
- Promotion: reply rate differs from the global observed rate by at least 15 points.
- Claim: association only. Never causal proof.
- Authority: no send, publish, purchase, LLM, or external mutation.
