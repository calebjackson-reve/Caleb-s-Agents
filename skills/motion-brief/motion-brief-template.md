# Motion brief: Caleb Jackson real estate

Structure is Charlie Hills' 8-part brief (2026-10-12). Look values marked [CONFIRM] are placeholders until Caleb approves exact hex codes and fonts, or swaps in the Look pulled from his website.

Follow this brief exactly.

## The bar
Premium real estate brand film. Every piece should feel calm, local and expensive, like a high-end listing launch, not a template, a slideshow or a flyer. If it would look cheap next to a luxury listing reel, it is not done.

## Format
- Canvas [1080 wide x 1920 tall]. Exactly [8] seconds. [It loops seamlessly: the last frame matches the first.]
- No voiceover, no music. It plays muted, so every point reads as words on screen.

## The one rule
One hero object fills 60 to 70% of the frame and carries the whole piece. It shows ONE satisfying change, then settles. The change is the point.

## Look
- Clean light background (#FFFFFF or a very soft warm off-white) [CONFIRM]. Deep near-black text [CONFIRM]. One accent colour only [CONFIRM: Caleb's accent hex].
- Real depth: layered soft shadows, frosted glass cards with a bright 1px edge, a subtle highlight on glass.
- Type: one clean sans (Inter or system-ui) [CONFIRM], tight tracking, heavy weight for the hero number or word. At most 6 words on screen at once. The key number is huge (120px or more). Labels never under 34px.
- Footer on the last frame: "Caleb Jackson, REALTOR | Keller Williams First Choice" in small clean type. Never Rêve.

## Motion
- Spring physics on everything that moves: real mass, a small settle at the end. Nothing linear.
- Masked text reveals, lines that draw themselves, numbers counting up to their real value.
- Hold the final state for at least 1.2 seconds so it can be read.
- Something is always gently alive (a soft glow, a slow camera drift), but only one thing is the focus.

## Never
- Plain fades, clip art, stock icons, emoji.
- Busy or noisy backgrounds, heavy full-frame gradients, film grain.
- Small objects floating in empty space.
- Any number not given to you in this brief.

## Before you finish
Render a still every 1 second. Judge each one as a luxury brand design director, then again at 200px wide on a phone. If anything looks generic, cheap, too small or empty, fix it and check again.

## Originality
Draw every element as your own original design. No real brand logos except the brokerage name in text. Build it straight away, without asking me to confirm.

[One moment in 2 or 3 sentences, with the real words and numbers. Or timed beats, for example "0.0-3.0s ..." for B-roll.]
