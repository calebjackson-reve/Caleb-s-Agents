# Nick Saraev's $25K/month roadmap

**Source:** Nick Saraev, Whimsical board "Roadmap to $25K/Month With Automation" (https://whimsical.com/roadmap-to-25k-month-with-automation-P5K6f1h9YpY5ngzvCW93Pg). Full transcription in `../ROADMAP-25K.md` in this folder. Read that file first.

## What Nick teaches

The board has two halves. The customer journey shows what a client experiences: six lead channels feed one funnel, one sales meeting doubles as discovery and close, a proposal with the agreement and payment built in, then kickoff, fulfillment, a recorded delivery video, revisions, completion, and a templated request for more work the moment the client is happiest. Unanswered follow-ups wait 30 days and loop back. Nick's rule: the client only sees deliverables, never the inside of the black box, so dress up the outside, and the period right after delivery is the highest ROI sales time you have. Recurring revenue is priority one.

The roadmap is 22 ordered steps. Steps 1 to 13 are foundations that take days, not months: niche, name, 5 case studies built from templates, domains, a 3-hour website, Google Workspace, 9 warmed mailboxes, a cold email tool, an Upwork profile, 3 communities with a 3x a week value-post habit, a basic CRM, a proposal template with payment at signing, Stripe. Step 14 is the engine: under 2 hours a day of sales every day (5 Upwork applications, three inbox and community passes). Steps 15 to 17 start after the first client: time everything, compute real $/hr, run a two-question retrospective, only then build project management. Steps 18 to 21 are scaling: rinse and repeat to $10K, then find which source produced the sales and 10x only that one, build infrastructure to handle the volume, root-cause every project success and failure, and push to $25K, which solo runs at 90%+ margin.

The ordering is the lesson. Nothing that is not sales gets built before the first customer. Misprioritization is why 90%+ of these businesses fail.

## Step by step

1. Read `../ROADMAP-25K.md` and identify which of the 22 steps Caleb is on. Do not skip ahead to tooling that belongs after the first client.
2. For the current step, pull the matching skills listed at the bottom of that file and apply them.
3. If there are zero paying clients for the offer, every plan ends with the step 14 daily sales block, scheduled on the calendar, under 2 hours, with the three touchpoints.
4. After any client, run the step 15 and 16 loop: hours logged, price charged, $/hr, what went well, what to improve. Record it in a retrospective file.
5. Once past $10K from the offer, run the step 19 analysis: sales by source and $/hr per source, then 10x the winner.

## Pitfalls Nick calls out

- Building a beautiful CRM or PM system with zero customers. Customers over admin.
- Pitching services in communities. Every post is value, the pitch happens in DMs after someone asks.
- Not timing projects. What you don't track won't improve, and you cannot price without real $/hr.
- Not asking for more work right after delivery. That window is the highest ROI sales time.
- Assuming $10K to $25K is more of the same. $0 to $10K is agility, $10K to $25K is systems.
- Growing a team because it feels like progress. Often less profitable than staying solo.
