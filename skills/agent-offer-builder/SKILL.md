# Agent offer builder

Read `aire/STACK.md`, `aire/rollout-2026-10/PLAN.md` section 5, `programs/ai-for-agents/PLAN.md` and `SKOOL-AI-FOR-AGENTS/STATUS.md` first. Use `ce-offer-formula` for the pitch and `cs-sop-to-skill` when the deliverable is a skill.

## Steps

1. **Name the system.** One sentence: what it does for an agent, measured how. If it has not run for real at least twice in Caleb's own business, say so and stop. Caleb sells what he uses.
2. **Draft three tiers.**
   - Free (Skool): a post or short lesson that teaches the idea and one step. Goal: trust and members. Draft the post in Caleb's voice, ready for `programs/ai-for-agents/`.
   - Workshop: a half-day or 90 minute outline under the existing ai-workshops program. Steps, what the agent sets up live, what they leave with.
   - Done-for-you: the caleb-skills plugin plus a STACK.md tuned to their brokerage, state rules and CRM. List exactly what gets installed and what stays Caleb's.
3. **Cost to deliver.** For each tier: Caleb hours, tool subscriptions the agent needs, and what breaks if their CRM or state differs. Label estimates as inference.
4. **Compliance.** Every tier's materials pass `ai-compliance-check`. Other states' rules are unknown: say so, and make the done-for-you tier include a state rules intake.
5. **Report.** The three tiers, the cost table, and the one tier to launch first with the reason. Pricing is a recommendation only. Caleb sets the price.

## Guardrails

- Nothing is posted to Skool, priced, or sold without Caleb's yes.
- Never promise results to agents. Use Caleb's own numbers with dates.
- No Rêve branding. Keller Williams First Choice only, and only where brokerage identity belongs.
- Voice: warm, direct, natural. No em dashes, no semicolons.
