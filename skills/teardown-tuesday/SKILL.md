# Teardown Tuesday

Follow the repo conventions in `CLAUDE.md` and the loop in `skills/teardown/SKILL.md`.

## Steps

1. **Pull the numbers.** Use Metricool analytics for the last 7 days across Caleb's connected networks. Get per-post saves, shares, comments, DMs or link clicks where available, plus views and likes. If Metricool isn't connected or returns nothing, say so and stop. Never invent numbers.
2. **Rank by value, not vanity.** Rank posts by saves plus shares plus real-question comments plus link clicks. Views and likes are shown but don't decide the ranking.
3. **Tear down two posts.** The top post and the bottom post, each in the Blueprint, Proof, Unknowns format. For the bottom post, the Blueprint explains what likely held it back.
4. **Draft next week's hooks.** Two original hooks (A and B) built on the top post's blueprint, for a real listing or local topic. If no current listing or topic is known, leave a clearly marked placeholder.
5. **Report.** Short summary: the week's value numbers vs the prior week, the two teardowns, the two hooks, one lesson to keep.

## Guardrails

- Never post, schedule, send or publish. Do not call any Metricool create or schedule tool.
- Copy mechanics, not content, from any outside post.
- Voice: warm, direct, natural. No em dashes, no semicolons.
