# conversation-actions — contract

**Business question.** Given a recent text or email conversation between Caleb and one person, what work does the conversation itself ask for: appointments, proposed times, deadlines, follow-ups, questions Caleb owes an answer to, promises either side made, documents or information someone owes, tasks Caleb agreed to, transaction steps, relationship follow-ups?

**Required inputs.** `conversation-context.v1` (brain/conversation_context.py: the bounded recent turns from both parties, unanswered questions, commitments, mentions, transactions and relationship outside the window, evidence ids), the person's active relationship roles (brain/relationship_roles.py), the message timestamps and the America/Chicago timezone. **Allowed sources.** Only those; the model never reads the database. **Tools.** None: one tool-free JSON-only turn through `brain/seats.py` (purpose `conversation_actions`, ceiling $0.50 per call) on the restricted paid first-party Anthropic route.

**Output.** `schema.json`: a bounded list of suggestions, each carrying the exact supporting excerpt, the evidence ids, the proposed date and time only when the words support one, an evidence class, why action is needed, and what is missing. **Deterministic checks after the model (code, brain/conversation_actions.py).** Every excerpt must be a verbatim substring of a turn in the context or the suggestion is rejected; every evidence id must be in the context; relative dates ("tomorrow", "next Friday") are re-resolved by the fail-closed resolver from the message timestamp in America/Chicago, and a proposed time the resolver cannot confirm is kept as an INFERENCE with the missing piece named; nothing is ever confirmed by the model. **Authority.** `prepares`: a suggestion is only ever a suggestion until Caleb edits, approves, dismisses, or marks it "not an action". Approval creates an internal task; a calendar event is staged only after Caleb confirms the exact calendar, title, date, time, timezone, location, notes, and attendees, and AIRE never invites anyone.

**Idempotency.** The suggestion key is derived from the person, the kind, the anchor message, and the normalized excerpt; an existing key (any state, including dismissed) is never re-created. Materially new evidence produces a new key. **Failure recovery.** Capped, failed, empty, or schema-invalid output produces no suggestions and a run receipt with the reason. **Evaluation cases.** tests/test_conversation_actions.py (fixture model): appointment with a resolvable relative date, vague "sometime next week" kept unconfirmed, invented excerpt rejected, duplicate run creates nothing, dismissed stays dismissed, email channel. **Promotion.** Caleb's decision with a receipt.
