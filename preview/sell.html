How I work

Priced straight. Marketed hard. Negotiated harder.

You'll hear from me directly at every decision, and you'll get what I actually think.

  1. Read the house honestly

    I walk it like a buyer will. What it does well, what they'll question, and what's worth fixing before it goes live. Some things are worth the money. Most aren't.

  2. Price it from the comps and the condition

    A number I can defend, with the recent sales that back it. We'll also talk about what the number means for your timing and your next move.

  3. Decide how it's seen

    Photography, the order of the rooms, the first line of the listing, all chosen on purpose. The Basil Lane example below is what that looks like in practice.

  4. Handle every offer with a plan

    When offers come in, you'll see the real trade-offs of each one in plain terms, then we negotiate. No guessing, no "let's see."

What to expect

Plain answers to the questions sellers ask first.

What do I get when I reach out?

A conversation, then a walk-through of the house if you want one, then a written read: what I'd price it at, why, what I'd fix first, and how I'd market it. No pressure to list.

Will you give me an instant valuation?

No. An instant number from a website is a guess. I'll give you a price backed by recent sales and the condition of your house, which takes a conversation and usually a visit.

How fast does it sell?

It depends on price, condition and the week. "Listed Tuesday, under contract Friday" has happened when a house is priced straight and shows well. I won't promise it for yours until I've seen it.

Where do you sell?

Baton Rouge, Zachary, St. Francisville, New Roads, the Felicianas, and across the river to Port Allen and Brusly, with closings in eleven parishes so far. See where I've sold on the map.
