    A sample you can use today

    Your showing game plan.

    1. Before you go: pick your three must-haves, monthly comfort range and one reason you would walk away.
    2. At the home: check how the rooms connect, storage, light, noise and everyday access. Record questions rather than guessing at defects.
    3. Before an offer: request disclosures, maintenance records and transaction-specific insurance, tax and flood information. Decide which inspections you need with qualified professionals.
    4. Afterward: write one strength, one tradeoff and the one unanswered question that matters most.

    No contact information is needed to use this plan.
