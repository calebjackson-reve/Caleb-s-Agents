## Three approaches compared

| | A. Own the north, seller-first, proof-led | B. Baton Rouge metro buyer volume | C. Statewide informational authority |
|---|---|---|---|
| Expected benefit | first measurable local pack and organic wins; listing appointments in Zachary, north EBR, East Feliciana | more traffic, mostly IDX, low conversion | citations in AI answers for Louisiana questions |
| Confidence | medium (thin competition observed; proof assets exist) | low (portals and volume teams own it) | medium-low (askdoss.com and national calculators incumbent; conversion weak) |
| Cost | $0 media; writing time | $0 media; large IDX content effort | writing time |
| Labor | 8 to 12 pages and GBP work over 90 days | 30+ area pages | 10 to 15 long guides |
| Dependencies | indexing confirmed; GBP decisions; tools verified | IDX template control | none special |
| Tradeoffs | narrower reach; Prairieville pin works against Maps in the north | diffuse; fights Zillow | visibility without local conversations |
| Fit | matches "Action Jackson" local proof and seller priority | weak fit | later layer |

Recommendation: **A**, with C's three statewide explainers (net proceeds, agent comparison, buyer-broker agreement) added only where they feed A's pages. Stop doing: unreviewed AI blog publishing; chasing aggregator "top agents" lists; any spend.

Why seller-first: sellers are the stated priority, the portal shows Caleb's listing history in Zachary and surrounding areas, the seller queries had no local agent page, and seller pages convert to listing appointments rather than IDX browsing. Current pipeline evidence in the mailbox shows both buyer and listing activity in September and October (aggregate only), so buyer and relocation content stays as the second lane.

## Targets and thresholds
Baseline is unknown, so numeric growth targets are deferred to the day-14 measurement milestone. Decision thresholds:
- Stop or rework a page if, after 60 days indexed, it has zero impressions for its principal query.
- Stop GBP posting experiments if 8 posts over 30 days produce no post views (Metricool) and no change in actions.
- Do not add geography while any priority page is unindexed.
- Any paid test stops at the first of: 30 days, no appointment, or the proposal CPA ceiling.

## Operating rules for future approved LP changes
Verify current save behavior first (typing can save); take before snapshots (screenshot and view-source); preview; keep locked homepage elements (Home Search and Home Valuation in the hero per the September tools plan); publish only with approval; test live output after saving; keep a rollback copy. Tracking changes: minimal design, synthetic validation only, no real submissions.
