# Competitor inventory, comparison matrix and battlecards

Method: competitors were discovered from 42 benchmark queries and 18 AI-style prompt runs in the WebSearch tool index on 2026-10-06 (US index, no location set, not a Google SERP, no Maps pack). No competitor page was fetched: this environment blocks all external hosts. Every attribute below is **source-reported from search snippets** unless marked observed (URL appeared). The battlecards therefore name 2 to 3 URLs per competitor as leads to review on the Mac, not as reviewed pages. Files: `csv/competitor-inventory.csv` (32 rows) and `csv/competitor-matrix.csv` (10 rows).

Selection rules: a competitor qualified if it appeared in a priority-market or statewide query, or was named in an AI-style recommendation run. Individual agents, teams, brokerages, portal publishers and informational substitutes are kept separate. Larger statewide names are benchmarks, not proof of local dominance.

## Scoring disclosure

The 0 to 5 "my assessment" score in the matrix weights: third-party authority citations (30%), observed presence on the exact queries Caleb wants (30%), local content depth (20%), review and GBP evidence (20%). It is my judgment from snippets. It is not a Google or AI ranking score, and most GBP and review cells are UNKNOWN.
