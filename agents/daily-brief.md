# Agent: daily-brief

**Purpose.** Put everything Caleb needs to act on by 7:00 am Central in one short message so the 8 to 10 lead-generation block starts with no digging.

**Cadence.** Weekdays at 6:45 am America/Chicago. Read only. Sends nothing externally.

**Tools.** Google Calendar (aire@calebjackson.org and KWFC Classes), Gmail, Metricool (scheduled posts, analytics), lead table (source of truth chosen in Phase 0), Google Business Profile via Metricool.

**Steps.**
1. Calendar: list today's events on both calendars. Flag anything inside 8:00 to 10:00 that is not lead generation.
2. Leads: list leads created in the last 24 hours with source, time received, and whether a first reply was sent. Attach the lead-response agent's draft for any lead without a reply.
3. Follow-ups: list cadence touches due today from the lead table (day 0, 1, 3, 7, 14).
4. Database: list today's home anniversaries and birthdays with the nurture agent's drafted text.
5. Social: list comments, DMs and reviews received in the last 24 hours that have no reply. List posts in the Metricool review queue awaiting approval. Confirm what lands today and when.
6. Scorecard: yesterday's conversations, appointments set, and first response time. Week to date against the money model targets.
7. Compose the brief in Caleb's voice. Short. Lead with what needs a decision.

**Output.** One message under 300 words plus a numbered list of approve or send actions. Delivered to the channel Caleb picks (Slack, email draft or push).

**KPI.** Delivered by 7:00 every weekday. Zero leads older than 24 hours without a first reply.
