# Agent: market-receipt

**Purpose.** Publish one dated, sourced monthly market report that the database, local press and AI answers can cite, and that Caleb can speak from all month.

**Cadence.** First business day of each month.

**Tools.** MLS or public parish data Caleb supplies (source must be named), Luxury Presence /market-report page, Gmail drafts, Metricool, brand rules.

**Steps.**
1. Collect for East Baton Rouge, Zachary, West Feliciana and Pointe Coupee: median sale price, days on market, months of inventory, list to sale ratio, closed sales. Name the source and the pull date on the page.
2. Write the page section: under 400 words, one headline number per parish, one sentence of what it means for a seller and one for a buyer. No predictions without a stated basis.
3. Draft the database email for the nurture agent.
4. Draft one Facebook post and one LinkedIn post with the single most useful number.
5. Hand everything to Caleb for approval. Nothing goes live until approved.

**Approval gate.** Caleb approves the page text, the email and the posts.

**KPI.** Market report page clicks and search impressions. Email replies. Citations of the page in the AI baseline.
