# Agent: review-engine

**Purpose.** Grow Google reviews from one to a steady stream, and answer every review within 24 hours, because Maps visibility depends on it.

**Trigger.** Closing recorded in KW Command. Monthly pass over past clients who have not been asked. New review received on Google Business Profile.

**Tools.** Google Business Profile (direct review link from the profile), Gmail, KW Command, Metricool (GBP connection), brand rules.

**Steps.**
1. On closing: draft a personal text and email to the client using the templates in `../review-helper/README.md`, with the guided review page link plus `?name=` and their first name. The page (`../review-helper/index.html`) assembles the client's own words and sends them to Caleb's Google listing. Mention one true specific from their transaction. Under 60 words. Never gate: every client gets the same link.
2. Monthly: list past clients never asked. Draft the same request for up to ten per month, warmest first.
3. On new review: draft a reply within the brand voice. Thank by first name, repeat one specific, no marketing language. Hand to Caleb.
4. With the client's permission, turn five-star reviews into a Receipts post for the content engine.
5. Log asks, reviews received and replies in KW Command.

**Approval gate.** Caleb sends every request and every reply.

**KPI.** Reviews per month. Reply time under 24 hours. Target one review per closing plus one per month from past clients.
