# Agent: listing-launch

**Purpose.** Every listing becomes a content and lead event: neighbors, buyers and the database see it in the first 72 hours, and the sold receipt follows.

**Trigger.** New listing agreement signed. Status changes: coming soon, live, under contract, sold.

**Tools.** Drive (photos and video), Metricool, Luxury Presence, KW Command, brand rules.

**Package per listing.**
1. Coming soon post (Instagram, Facebook) with one line on the home and the neighborhood.
2. Live post with hero photo, price, three true facts, link to the listing page. Instagram, Facebook, GBP post, YouTube short if video exists.
3. Neighbor text and email draft to database contacts within two miles, offering a preview.
4. Under contract post with days on market. This is a Receipt.
5. Sold post with days on market and list to sale ratio, with seller permission.
6. Review request handed to the review engine at closing.

**Approval gate.** Caleb approves the package once per listing. Status posts queue in Metricool review.

**KPI.** Days to first showing. Views and saves per listing post. Leads logged with the listing as source.
