## Searched and rejected

- Zachary: the only free photos are a paper mill (Ken Lund, CC BY-SA 2.0), a railroad depot (download kept failing), hurricane debris, and a flag. None fit a luxury site.
- Country Club of Louisiana, University Club Plantation, Bocage, Santa Maria: nothing on Wikimedia Commons, Flickr Creative Commons, Unsplash or Pexels. Flickr has 50 photos tagged University Club Plantation by Bill Cobb, all rights reserved, not usable without his permission. Golf course listing sites have photos with no license.
- St. Francisville Inn at dusk and the camellias: both 600px, too small.
- Pointe Coupee panoramio: a dirt lot by the water.
- Baton Rouge waterfront aerial from 1990: public domain but the skyline is 36 years old.
- Unsplash: the Baton Rouge search returns mostly New Orleans and Unsplash+ paid images. One free aerial by Leo_Visions exists but was not pulled.
