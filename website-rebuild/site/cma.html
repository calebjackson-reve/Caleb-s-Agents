CMA or appraisal?

Know which one you need.

A CMA is a licensed agent's read of the market, free and fast, built for pricing a sale or deciding whether to move. An appraisal is a licensed appraiser's signed report, ordered by your lender for a loan, or by you or your attorney for an estate, divorce or tax filing.
