## The project in one page

**Goal.** 100 free Skool members by day 90, every one a real agent who used AI for something real. Then one-on-ones, then a paid tier at $27 to $49 a month, then AIRE.

**Who it's for.** Agents starting at step 1. Caleb's roughly 500 agent friends on personal Facebook are the first 100 members. Louisiana first, then anyone.

**What makes it different.** Every other AI-for-agents group on Skool sells prompts and time savings. Nobody combines AI with the psychology of why clients decide. The closest group is sales psychology with no AI. Lead with that angle.

**The engine (copied from AI Automation Society, rebuilt for agents).**
1. A short video every week, each with a free download that only lives in Skool.
2. A 7-day challenge as the front door. Members post "Day 1," "Day 2" and so on, which keeps the feed alive.
3. Friday live Q&A. Questions become next week's lesson.
4. Free gets the lessons. Paid, later, gets Caleb.

**The 7-day challenge: 7 Days to Your First AI Assistant.**

| Day | Video | Length | They build |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | Welcome | 1:30 | Nothing. Say hi. |
| 1 | Your First Real Conversation | 4:30 | Their bio, rewritten |
| 2 | The Follow-Up They Answer | 4:30 | 3 follow-up texts |
| 3 | Ask Better Questions | 5:00 | 5 consult questions, Fair Housing safe |
| 4 | A Listing Video Script | 4:30 | A 60-second script |
| 5 | A Week of Posts in 20 Minutes | 4:00 | 5 posts |
| 6 | What Never Goes In | 3:30 | Their personal AI rules |
| 7 | Your Saved Assistant | 5:30 | A saved Claude Project or ChatGPT setup |

Lengths are the trimmed targets from the shot board. The scripts in `challenge/` still run longer and need a trim pass (see STATUS).

## How we film (decided 2026-10-12)

- **One spot, never changed.** A real room with 6 feet of depth behind Caleb. Window to one side at 45 degrees. Never a window behind him.
- **Gear.** iPhone rear camera, 4K at 30fps, tripod at eye level, 5 to 6 feet away. Clip-on wireless mic. Nothing bought without approval.
- **Screen.** Mac screen recording, browser at 125%, every tab and notification closed, zero client info on screen.
- **Style.** Face first, screen by 0:20, build the prompt live, zoom 150 to 200% on the prompt box, change the picture every 5 to 10 seconds, no intro, no logo, no music under explanations.
- **Order.** Day 1 is the test. Film it, cut it, watch it on a phone. Then one 4-hour batch day for all 8 challenge videos. Then one lesson every Tuesday, always one ahead.
- **Edit.** CapCut desktop. Steps are in `PRODUCTION.md`.
