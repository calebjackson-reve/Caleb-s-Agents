Fair Housing and safety check

Checked by Claude against the Day 1 script on 2026-10-12. This is a plain-language read, not legal advice. KW First Choice compliance has the final word.

What changed and why

  • Changed: the script's example "families moving up" is now "move-up buyers." Familial status is a protected class under the federal Fair Housing Act, and a bio is advertising. Describe the service, not who should live where.
  • Added to the prompt: "Describe what I do, not who should live where." It steers the AI away from phrases like "perfect for young families" or "great neighborhood for retirees."
  • Before you film: read your own result out loud. If it describes a person by race, color, religion, sex, disability, family status or national origin, or calls an area right for a type of person, cut that line.
  • On screen: no client names, numbers, addresses or chats. Only your own public bio details.
  • Brokerage: keep KW First Choice out of the typed bio and the frame until you have their okay.
